# Shareholders

A shareholder is a natural person (individual) or legal entity that deposits funds in exchange for ownership of the future company.
To deposit funds, the shareholder opens a temporary Swan payment account.

## Opening the temporary account {#account}

Because they're opening an account, each shareholder must go through the [onboarding](/accounts/guides/onboarding), [identification](/users/concepts/identifications), and [account holder verification](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/verification) processes.
Shareholders must also give proof of their residence address; only official documents are validated.
Refer to the list of acceptable documents in [proof of address](/accounts/reference/onboarding/proof-of-address).

After completing these requirements, the shareholder's account is assigned an <Term id="ibans">IBAN</Term> they can use to deposit funds.

A shareholder moves through several [statuses](/accounts/concepts/capital-deposits/statuses#shareholders) as they onboard and transfer funds.

Swan creates the temporary account once the shareholder completes onboarding. The account is limited in purpose and duration: it can only receive the shareholder's capital deposit funds, and Swan checks incoming transfers before forwarding the funds to the notary.
As soon as the `CapitalDepositCase` is complete, the shareholder's temporary account closes automatically.

## Transfer requirements {#transfer-requirements}

Each shareholder transfers their share of the capital to their temporary account. The transfer must:

- Come from an account held in the shareholder's name.
- Use a bank account accepted by Swan.
- Match the shareholder's expected capital deposit amount.

Transfers that don't meet these conditions may be rejected or returned. The shareholder has two attempts to transfer the funds correctly. If both attempts fail, the entire capital deposit case is canceled.

Shareholders can use an [Instant SEPA Credit Transfer](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/instant), which arrives immediately, or a SEPA Credit Transfer, which arrives according to standard interbank processing times, typically one business day.

:::caution Shareholder accounts only accept SEPA Credit Transfers
Capital can only be deposited into a shareholder account via [SEPA Credit Transfers](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/).
International credit transfers are rejected automatically before settlement, regardless of the amount.
Make sure all transfers are sent from the shareholder's account in their name.
:::
