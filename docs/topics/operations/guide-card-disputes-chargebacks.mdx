This guide helps you support your users through card disputes and chargebacks, including what evidence to collect and what to expect. It covers fraud disputes and ATM withdrawal cases for all Swan card types: virtual, physical, and digital. Swan does not currently process chargebacks for other commercial dispute types.

For card transactions outside the SEPA area, the disputed amount is the euro equivalent converted at Mastercard's exchange rate, as shown on the account statement. The dispute process is the same. For card transaction statuses and rejection codes, see [Card payments](https://docs.swan.io/topics/cards/payments/) on docs.swan.io. For insurance coverage, see [Card packages](https://docs.swan.io/topics/cards/card-packages/).

:::info Key takeaways
- Fraud disputes require a **signed fraudulent usage declaration**. A police report is strongly recommended.
- The chargeback process for ATM withdrawal disputes can take between **50 to 90 calendar days**.
- The number one cause of delays is **incomplete evidence** at initial submission.
- Swan processes chargebacks for **fraud disputes** and **ATM withdrawal** cases only. All other commercial disputes must be resolved directly with the merchant.
- A deductible of up to **€50** may apply to fraud refunds, as stated in the general Terms and Conditions.
:::

---

## Choose the right dispute path

Before collecting evidence, identify your user's role in the dispute:

- **Your user is the cardholder disputing an amount:** Follow the cardholder dispute process in this guide. Swan reviews the case, determines whether it is eligible for a chargeback, and refunds confirmed fraudulent transactions according to the applicable rules.
- **Your user is a merchant, and Swan received a chargeback request through Mastercard:** Submit a Swan Support request at [partner-support.swan.io](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new). Include the chargeback reference number and any documentation received from Mastercard.

:::caution Two separate processes
Don't use the cardholder fraud reimbursement process for merchant-side chargeback requests. These are separate paths with different evidence requirements, timelines, and outcomes.
:::

---

:::caution Prerequisite
Only transactions with a `Booked` status can be disputed. `Pending` card authorizations cannot be disputed.
:::

**Pre-authorization holds** such as hotel, Airbnb, or rental deposits that appear as Pending are not disputes. They release automatically when the merchant closes the authorization or after a set period. If your user has a hold that has not been released and the transaction is now showing as Booked, submit a Swan Support request at [partner-support.swan.io](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new).

## Follow the dispute process

| Step | Who | What happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Your user** | Reports the issue to your team with all relevant details. |
| **2** | **You** | Help your user gather the correct evidence before submission, then submit a Swan Support request at [partner-support.swan.io](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new) with all evidence attached. |
| **3** | **Swan** | Reviews the evidence and determines whether the dispute is eligible for a chargeback. |
| **4** | **Card network** | Processes the chargeback if filed. The merchant can accept or contest it. |
| **5** | **Swan** | Credits the account if the chargeback is successful. |

Not all disputes are eligible for a chargeback. Swan assesses each case based on the dispute type, the evidence provided, and the applicable regulatory and card network rules. If a dispute is not eligible, Swan will inform the user of the outcome and any alternative steps they can take.

## Dispute types

| Type | What it covers | Chargeback eligibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fraud** | Unauthorized transactions where card credentials were compromised, stolen, or used without the cardholder's authorization | Eligible. Swan files a chargeback with the card network after reviewing the evidence. |
| **ATM withdrawal** | Disputes where the cardholder did not receive the expected cash amount, or where the amount debited does not match the amount received | Eligible for ATM withdrawal disputes above €50 only. For all other commercial dispute types, the cardholder must attempt to resolve the issue directly with the merchant. Swan does not file chargebacks for other commercial scenarios. |

:::note Classification note
If your user was tricked into authorizing a payment (phishing, fake website), this is typically a commercial dispute, not fraud, even if it feels like fraud to the user. Submit a Swan Support request at [partner-support.swan.io](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new) to confirm the classification before advising your user to contact the merchant. Swan will confirm the appropriate next steps.
:::

## Collect evidence for a card fraud dispute

| Required evidence | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Police report** | Filed with local authorities. Strongly recommended for all fraud claims. In France, the official Perceval online fraud declaration can be submitted as an alternative or in addition to a police report. |
| **Signed fraudulent usage declaration** | Swan provides this form. The cardholder signs it to declare they did not authorize the transactions. Direct your user to the [end-user support article](https://support.swan.io/hc/en-150/articles/17099978406045) for the PDF in English and French. |
| **Government online fraud declaration** (if applicable) | For online fraud where the user still has their card, Swan may also ask them to complete the official government fraud declaration form and send the receipt. This is separate from the Swan form. Swan will request this during the investigation if applicable; your user doesn't need to submit it proactively. |
| **Transaction details** | Date, amount, and merchant name for each disputed transaction |
| **Screenshots** (optional) | From the banking app or account statement showing the disputed transactions |

A deductible of up to **€50** may apply to fraud refunds, as stated in the general Terms and Conditions. Swan informs the user whether this applies once the case has been fully analyzed.

:::info 3DS-authenticated transactions
In your support request, include whether the disputed transactions used 3DS authentication. When a transaction was completed with 3DS consent, the cardholder authenticated the payment at the time. This raises the bar for establishing that the transaction was unauthorized, and Swan may require additional evidence. Swan will advise on a case-by-case basis.
:::

The card must be permanently canceled, not just suspended. This prevents further unauthorized use and is required before Swan can process the dispute. Your user will need a replacement card. To cancel the card, instruct your user to cancel it via [Block your card](https://support.swan.io/hc/en-150/articles/18538920070045) on support.swan.io, or cancel it yourself via the Swan API. See [Cards](https://docs.swan.io/topics/cards/) on docs.swan.io for the API reference.

If your user has multiple disputed transactions on the same card, include all transactions in a single support request.

## Collect evidence for an ATM withdrawal dispute

Swan currently processes chargebacks for **ATM withdrawal** commercial disputes only. For all other commercial dispute types, direct your user to resolve the issue directly with the merchant.

Evidence requirements depend on the scenario. For ATM withdrawal disputes above €50, collect the required documents and submit a Swan Support request. For all other scenarios, the table below shows what evidence your user should gather when contacting the merchant directly.

:::caution Prerequisite for commercial disputes
For the commercial dispute scenarios in this table, the cardholder must have attempted to resolve the issue with the merchant for **at least 3 weeks** before a chargeback can be submitted. Evidence of this contact is required. This does not apply to fraud disputes or ATM withdrawal chargebacks.
:::

| Scenario | What the cardholder claims | Required evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product or service not received** | Paid but never received | Invoice or proof of purchase, delivery or shipping details with expected date, emails or screenshots showing contact with merchant |
| **Product damaged, defective, or not as described** | Product arrived but is wrong or damaged | Invoice or proof of purchase, photos of damaged goods |
| **Unrecognized charge** | Does not recognize the charge | No document required. If applicable: evidence of contact with merchant |
| **ATM withdrawal not received** | Did not receive funds during ATM withdrawal. Swan only processes ATM chargebacks for amounts above €50. | No document required |
| **Duplicate charge** | Charged multiple times for the same purchase | Invoice or proof of purchase, evidence of contact with merchant |
| **Wrong amount charged** | Amount debited does not match invoice | Invoice or proof of purchase, evidence of contact with merchant |
| **Refund not credited** | Merchant confirmed a refund, but it has not appeared | Invoice or proof of purchase, emails where the merchant confirmed the refund and amount |

:::tip Canceled orders, reservations, and subscriptions
If your user canceled an order, booking, or subscription and hasn't received a refund, this falls under **Refund not credited** if the merchant confirmed the cancellation and refund in writing, or **Product or service not received** if no cancellation confirmation exists. Collect evidence of the cancellation alongside the standard evidence for that scenario.
:::

## Understand expected timelines

These timelines apply when a chargeback is filed. Not all disputes result in a chargeback.

:::caution Filing deadline
For users acting for **non-professional purposes**, they have **13 months** from the debit date to dispute the transaction. For users acting for **professional purposes**, the timeframe is **8 weeks**. Disputes submitted after this window are not eligible for a chargeback under card network rules.
:::

Swan cannot accelerate the card network's processes. Set expectations with your users early.

Fraud dispute timelines vary depending on the complexity of the case and whether additional evidence is requested. There's no fixed resolution window. Follow up via the open support ticket if you haven't received an update after 10 business days.

## Possible outcomes

:::info Tracking disputes
Partners can't currently track dispute progress directly in Dashboard. To check the status of an open dispute, follow up via the existing support ticket. Swan's teams track and update dispute cases internally.
:::

:::note ATM chargebacks
The only exception is for ATM chargebacks, where the timeline may differ and could potentially be shared separately.
:::

| Outcome | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Chargeback successful** | Funds are credited to the user's account. According to the T&Cs or applicable law, this should be done within 10 business days. If the credit has not appeared after several weeks of Swan confirming the outcome, submit a Swan Support request referencing the original dispute ticket. |
| **Chargeback denied** | The merchant provided valid proof, or the evidence did not meet card network requirements. Chargeback decisions issued by the card network cannot be appealed. If your user has new evidence that was not previously submitted, submit a Swan Support request. Swan will advise on whether any further steps are possible. |
| **Dispute not eligible for chargeback** | The case does not meet the criteria for a chargeback under the applicable rules. Swan advises the user on alternative steps. |
| **Merchant contests** | Swan may ask for additional evidence. Respond as quickly as possible, as delays can affect the outcome. If your user cannot supply the requested evidence, include an explanation in your reply and Swan will advise on alternative steps. |
| **Transaction fee refunds** | For cardholder-side disputes, transaction fees associated with confirmed fraudulent transactions are refunded, but they may not be refunded automatically and may require separate processing. This does not apply to merchant-side chargeback requests received through Mastercard. |

## Check insurance eligibility

Card insurance is available on Standard, Essential, or Premium card packages only, and only when the user's account membership status is `Enabled` and verification status is `Verified`.

If Swan is unable to reimburse a fraud dispute and you believe your user may be covered by card insurance, submit a Swan Support request and specify that you are requesting an insurance referral. Swan will confirm eligibility and initiate the insurance claims process. Insurance claims follow a separate process from disputes and are not handled through the standard dispute workflow. For coverage details, see [Card packages](https://docs.swan.io/topics/cards/card-packages/) on docs.swan.io.

## Contact Swan Support

Submit a Swan Support request at [partner-support.swan.io](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new).

Contact Swan Support if:

- Your user is unable to cancel their card and **immediate card blocking** is needed.
- It has been more than **10 business days** since evidence submission with no update.
- Your user received an evidence request they do not understand.
- You need the status of an ongoing dispute.
- You are unsure whether a case qualifies as fraud or a commercial dispute.
- The merchant confirmed a payment did not go through, but your user's account was still charged. Include written confirmation from the merchant in your request.
- Your user needs a formal attestation that a dispute has been filed.

Include in your request:

- Account ID
- Card details (first 6 and last 4 digits only, never the full number)
- Transaction date, amount, and merchant name
- Dispute type (fraud or commercial) and which scenario applies
- Any previous reference numbers
