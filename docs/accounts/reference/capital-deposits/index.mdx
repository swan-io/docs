# Capital deposit reference

<p className="ia-lede">Cancelation reason codes, document refusal reasons, and required documents for <Term id="capital-deposit">capital deposit</Term> cases.</p>

For the case, shareholder, and document state machines, see [capital deposit statuses](/accounts/concepts/capital-deposits/statuses).

## Cancelation reason codes {#cancelation-reason-codes}

When a capital deposit case has the status `Canceled`, a reason code is available on the [`CapitalDepositCaseCanceledStatusInfo`](https://api-reference.swan.io/objects/capital-deposit-case-canceled-status-info) type.

:::note Retroactive migration
Capital deposit cases canceled before 2 April 2026 were migrated retroactively to the current cancelation reason codes in June 2026, so every canceled case carries a reason code.
:::

| Reason code | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| `KYCIssue` | Documents are missing or incorrect, or the requested information wasn't sent. |
| `ForbiddenActivity` | The company operates in a prohibited business sector. |
| `DuplicateOnboarding` | A second account was created by mistake. |
| `DeniedCountryResidency` | A beneficial owner (UBO) or resident is located in a restricted country. |
| `RefusedIbanCountry` | The country associated with the IBAN isn't supported. |
| `CompanyRegistrationCountryNotSupported` | The company's country of registration isn't supported. |
| `BusinessInactiveOrNotRegistered` | The company is inactive or not registered. |
| `UnsupportedLegalForm` | Swan can't onboard this legal form. |
| `TCViolation` | The account holder violated the terms and conditions. |
| `CapitalDepositWithdrawn` | The capital deposit was withdrawn. |
| `CapitalDepositRefusedByNotary` | The notary refused the capital deposit case. |
| `ComplianceReason` | The case was canceled for compliance reasons. |
| `RegulatoryRequirement` | The case was canceled because of a regulatory requirement. |

These reason codes come from the dedicated `CapitalDepositCaseCancellationReasonType` enum.
Most values are shared with [account closure reason codes](/accounts/concepts/closure/reason-codes), which apply at the account level.
When a capital deposit completes successfully, the case isn't canceled; instead, shareholder accounts are closed with the account-level reason code `CapitalDepositCompleted`.

## Document refusal reasons {#document-refusal-reasons}

When a capital deposit document is [`Refused`](/accounts/concepts/capital-deposits/statuses#documents), a reason code is available on the [`CapitalDepositDocumentRefusedStatusInfo`](https://api-reference.swan.io/objects/capital-deposit-document-refused-status-info) type, with an optional plain-text `reason` adding details.

These reason codes come from the dedicated `DocumentReasonCode` enum.
[Supporting documents](/accounts/concepts/documents/statuses#refused) use a different enum for refusal reasons.

| Reason code | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| `CompanyNameMismatch` | The company name in the document submitted doesn't match Swan's records. |
| `DeclaredAmountMismatch` | The declared amount in the document submitted doesn't match Swan's records. |
| `ExpiredDocument` | Documents must have been issued within the last three months. |
| `InvalidAddress` | The address in the document submitted doesn't match Swan's records. |
| `InvalidDocument` | The document submitted isn't valid or in an accepted format. |
| `InvalidOrMissingData` | The name in the document submitted is invalid, missing, or not clearly visible. |
| `MissingAccommodationProviderId` | An identity document for the accommodation provider is required. |
| `MissingAccommodationProviderIdLetter` | The accommodation provider's ID and letter providing proof of accommodation are required. |
| `MissingAccommodationProviderLetter` | A letter providing proof of accommodation from the provider is required. |
| `UnacceptableDocument` | The document submitted doesn't match the requested document type. |
| `ProviderColorIdDocumentRequired` | The document must be submitted in color. |
| `MissingSignature` | The document submitted must be signed or dated. |
| `BadDocumentQuality` | The quality of the document submitted is too low. All document content must be clear and legible. |
| `FullDocumentRequired` | The entire document must be submitted, not just a portion of the document. |
| `MissingProviderProofOfAddress` | The accommodation provider's proof of address is required. |
| `MissingProviderProofOfAddressAndIdDocument` | The accommodation provider's ID and proof of address are required. |
| `PowerOfAttorneyToSwanRequired` | A signed and dated Power of Attorney must be provided to Swan. |
| `MissingCompanyDomiciliationStatement` | A document stating the company's registered address is required. This document can be referred to as a "domiciliation statement". |
| `HandwrittenOrCertifiedElectronicSignatureRequired` | The signature must be handwritten or electronically certified. |
| `ComplianceReason` | The document was refused for compliance reasons. |
| `InvalidDepositBankName` | The bank name in the articles of association document is invalid. |
| `MissingCompanyProviderRegisterExtract` | The register extract of the company providing the accommodation and an identity document for its legal representative are required. |

## List of required documents {#documents-list}

The following table lists the documents that may be required. You upload some documents, Swan collects others during onboarding and <Term id="identification">identification</Term>, and the notary uploads the capital share deposit certificate.

| Stakeholder | Document | API document type | How it is collected | Attached to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Individual shareholder | [Proof of address](/accounts/reference/onboarding/proof-of-address) | `ProofOfIndividualAddress` | You upload it with `generateCapitalDepositDocumentUploadUrl`. | `shareholders[].documents` |
| Individual shareholder | Proof of identity | `ProofOfIdentity` | Swan collects it during identification after onboarding is finalized. | The account holder's [supporting document collection](/accounts/concepts/documents) |
| Company shareholder | Commercial registry extract issued within the last three months | `RegisterExtract` | Swan collects it during company onboarding. | The account holder's [supporting document collection](/accounts/concepts/documents) |
| Company shareholder | Proof of identity for the <Term id="legal-representative">legal representative</Term> | `ProofOfIdentity` | Swan collects it during identification when the legal representative is the account administrator. | The account holder's [supporting document collection](/accounts/concepts/documents) |
| Future company | Draft articles of incorporation | `ArticlesOfIncorporation` | You upload it with `generateCapitalDepositDocumentUploadUrl`. | `capitalDepositCase.documents` |
| Future company | Lease agreement for the registered office | `CompanyLeaseAgreement` | You upload it with `generateCapitalDepositDocumentUploadUrl`. | `capitalDepositCase.documents` |
| Future company | Power of attorney from the legal representative to Swan | `PowerOfAttorney` | You upload it with `generateCapitalDepositDocumentUploadUrl`. | `capitalDepositCase.documents` |
| Future company | Capital share deposit certificate | `CapitalShareDepositCertificate` | The notary uploads it. | `capitalDepositCase.documents` |
| Future company | Commercial registry extract, or KBIS, issued after incorporation | `RegisterExtract` | You upload it with `generateCapitalDepositDocumentUploadUrl` after incorporation. | `capitalDepositCase.documents` |

### Registered office without a lease agreement {#without-lease}

If the future company doesn't have a lease agreement as proof of its official company address, send the documents required for its situation.

All identity documents need to be in full color and include the front and back of the document.

| Official company address | Additional documents |
| --- | --- |
| The legal representative's address | Business address affidavit (domiciliation statement) from the legal representative. If the legal representative isn't a shareholder, also send their identity document and proof of address. |
| The legal representative's address, but the representative rents the property or is hosted by someone else | Business address affidavit (domiciliation statement) from the host. If the legal representative isn't a shareholder, also send the legal representative's identity document, the host's identity document, and the host's proof of address. |
| Another company's office address | KBIS for the host company issued within the last three months, a business address affidavit (domiciliation statement) from the host company's legal representative, and the identity document of the legal representative who gave the affidavit. |
