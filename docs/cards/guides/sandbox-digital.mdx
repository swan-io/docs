# Sandbox: digital cards

Simulate adding a <Term id="cards-digital">digital card</Term> to Apple Pay or Google Pay in your Sandbox project.

## Simulate manually provisioning a digital card {#simulate-manual}

1. On your Dashboard, go to **Developers** > **Event Simulator**.
1. Open **Cards**, then **Apple Pay / Google Pay**.
1. Go to the **Manual provisioning** tab, enter the required fields, then click **Simulate**.

## Simulate finalizing in-app provisioning of a digital card {#simulate-in-app}

1. On your Dashboard, go to **Developers** > **Event Simulator**.
1. Open **Cards**, then **Apple Pay / Google Pay**.
1. Go to the **Finalize InApp Provisioning** tab, enter the required fields, then click **Simulate**.

:::info Google Wallet in Sandbox
In Sandbox, `inAppProvisioningData` returns a mock `googleOpaquePaymentCard` when you pass `googleWallet`.
Use it to test the API and your error handling.
Google won't accept the mock value, so the card can't be added to Google Wallet from Sandbox.
:::
