# Merchant payment methods

<p className="ia-lede">A <Term id="merchant-payment-method">merchant payment method</Term> is one way a merchant can be paid, attached to their profile and reviewed by Swan before it's enabled. A merchant needs an enabled profile and at least one enabled method to accept payments.</p>

import MerchantPaymentMethodDefinition from '../../../_shared/definitions/_merchant-payment-method.mdx';

> <MerchantPaymentMethodDefinition />

Your merchants can accept customer payments using several payment methods.

Merchant payment methods are connected to a [merchant profile](/payments/concepts/merchants/profiles). A single merchant profile can have multiple payment methods.
To accept payments with Swan, there must be an `Enabled` merchant profile and at least one `Enabled` merchant payment method.

To learn how to request payment methods, refer to the request payment method guides for [card payments](/payments/guides/merchants/online/cards/request), [SEPA Direct Debit](/payments/guides/merchants/online/sdd/request), [Internal Direct Debit](/payments/guides/merchants/online/idd/request), [in-person cards](/payments/guides/merchants/in-person/cards/request-method), and [French checks](/payments/guides/merchants/in-person/checks/request).
You can request multiple methods in one API call.

## Available payment methods {#methods-options}

Merchant payment methods are enabled per Partner: Swan reviews your use case before your merchants can request them (see the [pending review note](/payments/concepts/merchants#overview) on the Merchants hub). <Term id="rolling-reserve">Rolling reserve</Term>, payment amount limits, and how the merchant's name appears on statements are described on the [merchant payments](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments) page.

All merchant payment method requests are subject to a [risk review](/payments/concepts/merchants#risk) and start with the status `PendingReview`. 
As part of this process, Swan may need to request additional information from the merchant to complete the assessment.

## Payment method statuses {#method-statuses}

The full lifecycle, including `WaitingForInformation`, and the rejection reasons are on the [merchant statuses](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#method-statuses) page.

## Versions {#methods-versions-statuses}

Swan creates a **new version** of your payment method **every time an update is approved**.
Only one version of a payment method can have the status `Enabled`.

Swan manages the statuses for `Enabled` or `PendingReview` payment methods automatically.
Therefore, you don't need to change the status before calling the API.
