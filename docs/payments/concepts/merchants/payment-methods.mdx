# Merchant payment methods

<p className="ia-lede">A <Term id="merchant-payment-method">merchant payment method</Term> is one way a merchant can be paid, attached to their profile and reviewed by Swan before it's enabled. A merchant needs an enabled profile and at least one enabled method to accept payments.</p>

import MerchantPaymentMethodDefinition from '../../../_shared/definitions/_merchant-payment-method.mdx';

> <MerchantPaymentMethodDefinition />

A single [merchant profile](/payments/concepts/merchants/profiles) can have multiple payment methods.
You can request several payment methods in one API call.

## Available payment methods {#methods-options}

- [Online cards](/payments/concepts/merchants/online-cards): [request guide](/payments/guides/merchants/online/cards/request)
- [SEPA Direct Debit](/payments/concepts/merchants/sepa-direct-debit) (Core and B2B): [request guide](/payments/guides/merchants/online/sdd/request)
- [Internal Direct Debit](/payments/concepts/merchants/internal-direct-debit) (Standard and B2B): [request guide](/payments/guides/merchants/online/idd/request)
- [In-person cards](/payments/concepts/merchants/in-person-cards): [request guide](/payments/guides/merchants/in-person/cards/request-method)
- [French checks](/payments/concepts/merchants/french-checks): [request guide](/payments/guides/merchants/in-person/checks/request)

Merchant payment methods are [enabled per Partner](/payments/concepts/merchants#overview) before your merchants can request them. <Term id="rolling-reserve">Rolling reserve</Term>, payment amount limits, and how the merchant's name appears on statements are described on the [merchant payments](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments) page.

Every request is subject to Swan's [risk review](/payments/concepts/merchants#risk) and starts as `PendingReview`. Swan may ask for more information before deciding, even if the merchant profile is already `Enabled`, contacting you or your user according to your communication preferences.

## Payment method statuses {#method-statuses}

The full lifecycle, including `WaitingForInformation`, and the rejection reasons are on the [merchant statuses](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#method-statuses) page.

## Versions {#methods-versions-statuses}

Swan creates a **new version** of your payment method **every time an update is approved**.
Only one version of a payment method can have the status `Enabled`.

Swan manages the statuses for `Enabled` or `PendingReview` payment methods automatically.
Therefore, you don't need to change the status before calling the API.
