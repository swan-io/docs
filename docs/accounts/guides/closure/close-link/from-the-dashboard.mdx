# Send a link to close an account from the Dashboard

Send the account's <Term id="legal-representative">legal representative</Term> a Dashboard link so they can complete account closure themselves.

import SuspendedCaution from '../partials/_suspended-caution.mdx';

<SuspendedCaution />

## Step 1: Send the link {#send-link}

1. Go to your **Dashboard** > **Data** > **Accounts**.
1. Locate the account closure link in the **Close account** tile.
1. **Click-to-copy** the link.
1. Send the link to the account's legal representative.

:::note Tracking closure
Keep track of the account closure process on **Dashboard** > **Data** > **Accounts** as the [account status](/accounts/concepts/account/statuses) changes.
:::

## Step 2: Legal representative completes the process {#complete-process}

The account's legal representative opens the link you give them, then logs into their banking app.
They choose a reason for account closure, then consent to closing the account with [Strong Customer Authentication](/users/concepts/consent/sca).

To complete the process, the [account balance must reach zero](/accounts/concepts/closure/processes#closure-legal-rep); the account is then closed within 30 days.
