# Card design

<p className="ia-lede">The look of your cards everywhere cardholders see them: standard designs, custom designs, and packaging.</p>

Your card design appears several times during your cardholders' user experience.
Your design is:

- Featured in the Web Banking interface for <Term id="cards-virtual">virtual</Term> and <Term id="cards-physical">physical</Term> cards
- Printed on physical cards
- Displayed in digital wallets

Each card design belongs to a [card product](/cards/concepts/card-products).

## Standard card design {#standard}

Swan proposes standard card designs that are already validated by Mastercard: black and silver plastic cards, plus a metal card available with the Premium package.

Personalize standard cards with your logo on the front of the card.
See it now in Swan's [card design studio](https://swan-io.github.io/card-design-studio/).

### Black {#black}

Black cards are made of black recycled plastic with a sleek matte finish and feature your logo in silver monochrome.
There are additional particles added to the plastic to minimize scratches.

![Image of the front and back of the standard black card design with the partner logo in silver](../../images/cards/cards-design-standard-black.png)

### Silver {#silver}

Silver cards feature your logo in black monochrome.

![Image of the front and back of the standard silver card design with the partner logo in black](../../images/cards/cards-design-standard-silver.png)

### Metal {#metal}

Metal cards are available with the `Premium` card package. The cards are made from stainless steel and tungsten and weigh 22 grams. They feature a textured dark protective layer on each side and exposed metal edges. Your logo is laser-printed on the cards, revealing the metal underneath.

## Custom card design {#custom}

If the standard designs don't meet your needs, or you want to customize more to your brand's style, Swan also offers [custom card designs](/cards/guides/design/custom).
The process includes both your PIM (Product Integration Manager) and your general Account Manager.
