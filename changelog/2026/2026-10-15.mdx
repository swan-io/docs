## Improvements

🌍 **Birth postal code is now only required in France.** The birth postal code is no longer required outside France:

- [Company onboarding](/topics/onboarding/company/fields#individual-birth-information): for ultimate beneficial owners and legal representatives, it's no longer required when the account country isn't France.
- [User sign-up](/topics/users#signup): it's only asked when the user was born in France. You no longer need to collect or [prefill `birthPlacePostalCode`](/developers/using-api/authentication/guide-get-token-user#url-parameters-optional) for users born outside France.

Birth date, city, and country are unchanged.

## API updates

### Upcoming breaking changes

🌍 **Territory codes will no longer be accepted in country fields.** From 12 November 2026, country fields (birth, address, ID document issuing, and company registration country) will no longer accept territory codes such as `REU` (Réunion). Use the corresponding country code instead (`FRA`). Existing records will be updated the same way. Onboardings whose account holder lives [in an area where Swan doesn't offer accounts](/partnership/overview/country-coverage) will be `Invalid`. Other country fields, such as card delivery addresses, aren't affected.

🐣 **New birth date rules for legal representatives, account admins, and ultimate beneficial owners.** From 12 November 2026, onboardings will apply new birth date rules:

- Account admins of [company](/topics/onboarding/company/fields#administrator-birth-information) and [individual](/topics/onboarding/individual/fields#birth-information) accounts must be 18 or older. This also applies to verification renewals and account admin changes.
- [Legal representatives](/topics/onboarding/company/fields#individual-birth-information) (`LegalRepresentative` or `LegalRepresentativeAndUltimateBeneficialOwner`) must be 18 or older. A legal representative under 18 will be rejected. There's no maximum age.
- Ultimate beneficial owners (`UltimateBeneficialOwner`) can be any age, including minors. This also applies to verification renewals.

A birth date in the future is still rejected. Account members aren't affected.

🔝 **Funding limits deprecation.** We're deprecating `fundingLimitSettings` and `fundingLimitSettingsChangeRequests`. They belong to a product Swan no longer offers. The following are deprecated accordingly:

- Mutation: `addFundingLimitSettingsChangeRequest`.
- Queries: `fundingLimitSettingsChangeRequests`, `AccountHolder.fundingLimitSettings`, and `AccountHolder.fundingLimitSettingsChangeRequests`.
- Simulation: `updateFundingLimitSettingsChangeRequest`.

They'll be removed entirely at the end of November 2026.

🔌 **AIS: Origin Transaction ID replaces Payment ID.** From 12 November 2026, the `extra.additional_information` field in Account Information Service (AIS) transaction responses returns the Origin Transaction ID (`originTransactionId`) instead of the Payment ID (`paymentId`). This makes it easier to reconcile fee transactions in accounting tools such as DATEV in Germany. The Payment ID will no longer be available through AIS. If your integration relies on it, contact your technical account manager. See the [AIS field mapping](/topics/open-banking/ais#field-mapping).
