## New features

💳 `inAppProvisioningData` has a new `googleWallet` argument to support **Google Unified Push Provisioning**. Call it from the Google Wallet SDK's `PaymentCredentialsGenerator.generate()` callback with your `googleConsoleId` and the `serverSessionId` it provides, and the response includes the Google Opaque Payment Card (`googleOpaquePaymentCard`) to return to the SDK. It's available in production on request, once your Google console is set up with Swan. Swan will update the partner guide for [in-app provisioning](/topics/cards/digital/#integration) with full integration instructions.

## Improvements

🔎 You can now view and filter [merchant payment methods](/topics/merchants/) from your Dashboard. The new **Payment methods** tab under **Dashboard** > **Merchants** lists all payment methods, with filters by status, type, and merchant ID.

## API updates

### Breaking changes

🇫🇷 For French companies, Swan no longer prefills ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs) and legal representatives from the company registry, to limit the personal data it shares. Collect them from your user and send them in the onboarding yourself. Company information (name, legal form, address, and so on) is still retrieved.

- [Legacy onboarding](/preview/new-onboarding/legacy-onboarding/company#retrieve-data): no people are prefilled anymore, whether individuals or companies acting as legal representatives.
- [Current onboarding](/preview/new-onboarding#company-registry-data): `companyInfoRegistryData` returns `relatedIndividuals` empty. `relatedCompanies` is still returned.

### Upcoming breaking changes

📅 On **1 December 2026**, `inAppProvisioningData` will refuse a Google Pay digital card queried without `googleWallet`, returning `BAD_USER_INPUT`, as Google ends legacy push provisioning at the end of December. If you add cards to Google Wallet from your own app, migrate to Unified Push Provisioning before this date. Apple Pay and manual provisioning aren't affected.

### Reminders

👀 On **12 November 2026**, three changes [announced on 15 October](/changelog/15-october-2026#upcoming-breaking-changes) take effect:

- Territory codes (for example, `REU`) are no longer accepted in country fields. Use the country code instead (`FRA`).
- New birth date rules apply to legal representatives, account admins, and ultimate beneficial owners.
- The Account Information Service (AIS) returns the Origin Transaction ID instead of the Payment ID in `extra.additional_information`.
