# Manage verification renewal (Re-KYC)

Guide your account holders through [verification renewal](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/verification-renewal) in Swan's no-code, partner-branded portal, and handle the surrounding tasks: communication preferences, branding, and lifting a suspension.

:::tip Prerequisites
Make sure your [branding settings](/get-started/set-up-swan/create-project#step-8-configure-your-branding-in-live) (logo and accent color) are up to date in your **Dashboard** > **Settings** > **Branding**.
The portal is partner-branded, so your users see your logo and colors.
:::

## Set your communication preferences {#communication-preferences}

We send the initial form and all follow-ups based on your [communication preferences](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/notifications).
We recommend that Swan emails your end users directly and copies you (CC). You can also specify a unique email address.

Create a [support ticket](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new) that outlines your communication preferences for verification renewal.

## Portal steps {#steps}

The <Term id="legal-representative">legal representative</Term> (account admin):

1. Receives the initial notification email with a unique portal link ([timeline](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/verification-renewal#verification-renewal-timeline)).
1. Opens the link and gives consent to access the portal.
1. Reviews the pre-filled information.
1. Confirms the information is correct, or updates it if anything has changed.
1. Uploads [supporting documents](/accounts/concepts/documents#required-documents) if required.
1. Submits the form and receives a confirmation email.

The <Term id="kyc">KYC</Term> analyst reviews the submission.
If more information is needed, the KYC analyst sends a follow-up request with a new portal link.
The portal reopens for the missing items only.

Share [Swan's Support Center article](https://support.swan.io/hc/en-150/articles/26326687003549-Confirm-personal-details-again) to show your users the full timeline and the reminders we send.

## Lift a suspension {#lift-suspension}

If the account holder misses the 90-day deadline and their accounts are suspended:

1. The user completes the process using the last portal link Swan emailed them.
1. Afterwards, either you or the user must submit a [support request](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new) for a review.
1. The KYC analyst reviews the suspension reason and the updated information.
