# SEPA Credit Transfers

<p className="ia-lede">Transfer euros between accounts located in the Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA), as regular, instant, internal, or recurring transfers.</p>

## Overview {#overview}

Note the acronyms; while the documentation doesn't use them, the acronyms are sometimes present in the API and used in written and verbal communication.

| Transfer type | Description | Acronym |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEPA Credit Transfers | Transfers up to 1 cent less than a billion euros within [SEPA](/payments/reference/sepa#sepa) that take up to 48 business hours to complete | SCT |
| [Instant SEPA Credit Transfers](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/instant) | Transfers up to 100,000 euros within SEPA that are executed in 10 seconds | SCT Inst |
| Swan Internal Credit Transfers | Transfers between two Swan accounts in the same project. They work like SEPA Credit Transfers, share the `SEPA Credit Transfer` payment product on **Dashboard** > **Data** > **Accounts** > **Transactions**, and are almost as quick as instant transfers | None |
| [Standing Orders](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/standing-orders) | Scheduled, recurring transfers within SEPA that can be instant or regular | None |

If you see the acronym **ICT**, it probably refers to [International Credit Transfers](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/international), not Swan Internal Credit Transfers.
Every transfer can carry a [label, reference, and invoice reference](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/label-and-reference).

### Consent {#consent}

Initiating credit transfers is a [sensitive operation](/users/reference/sensitive-operations) and requires <Term id="consent">consent</Term> from an account member with the `canInitiatePayments` [membership permission](/accounts/reference/memberships/membership-permissions#permissions).

### Risk and credit transfers {#risk}

All credit transfers pass through an automated risk assessment.
If the automated assessment flags the transfer, it triggers a manual review by Swan.

If one of your transactions is flagged, Swan might contact you for more information.
Due to the manual review, flagged SEPA Credit Transfers take longer to execute.

### Verifying beneficiaries {#beneficiary-verification}

Swan runs [Verification of Payee](/payments/concepts/verification-of-payee) on every outgoing SEPA Credit Transfer and answers [incoming requests](/payments/concepts/verification-of-payee/incoming-requests) for Swan accounts.

Swan also **validates the format** of all beneficiary IBANs.
You can [check the format of your IBANs](/accounts/guides/ibans/validate) with the API before using them to send a transfer.

### Transfer limitations {#limitations}

1. Credit transfers can't be sent if [**account holder verification**](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/verification) is still **in progress**.
While finalizing verification, the account's `paymentLevel` is `Limited` and the `initiateCreditTransfers` mutation returns an error.
1. Swan accounts aren't designed to have a **negative balance**. Therefore, Swan checks an account's `Available` balance before executing credit transfers every time. If there isn't enough in the account, the transfer is rejected for insufficient funds.
1. If you'd like to plan credit transfers in advance, you can do so **up to one year in the future** using the `requestedExecutionAt` input when initiating your transfer.
1. Incoming transfers to a `Limited` account are capped: refer to [limited account restrictions](/accounts/concepts/account/type-and-level#limited-account-restrictions).

## Instant SEPA Credit Transfers {#instant}

When an instant transfer can't go through, a [fallback mode](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/instant#instant-fallback) decides whether it becomes a regular transfer or is abandoned.
Refer to [Instant SEPA Credit Transfers](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/instant).

## Standing Orders {#standing-orders}

Standing Orders are scheduled, recurring credit transfers, sent as regular or instant transfers with a fixed amount or a target balance.
Refer to [Standing Orders](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/standing-orders).
