# Physical cards

<p className="ia-lede">An optional plastic card ordered for a <Term id="cards-virtual">virtual card</Term>, usable for swipe, chip and PIN, contactless, and online payments.</p>

<PhysicalCardsDefinition />

You can order a physical card for each virtual card.
Your [design](/cards/concepts/design) is printed on all physical cards.

## Printing physical cards with the API {#mutations}

You can use one of several mutations to print physical cards with the API.

| Mutation | Use case | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`printPhysicalCard`](https://api-reference.swan.io/mutations/print-physical-card/) | Print one physical card for one <Term id="account-membership">account member</Term>. The [virtual card](/cards/concepts/virtual) must already be added. | [Print a physical card](/cards/guides/physical/print#print-one) |
| [`addCards`](https://api-reference.swan.io/mutations/add-cards/) | <AddCardsUseCases /> | [Print a physical card when adding a virtual card](/cards/guides/physical/print#print-with-virtual) |
| [`addCardsWithGroupDelivery`](https://api-reference.swan.io/mutations/add-cards-with-group-delivery/) | Ship a group of physical cards together to the same address. | [Print multiple physical cards with group delivery](/cards/guides/physical/print#print-group) |

## Card number {#number}

Each physical card has a unique card number, different even from the virtual card added before the physical card was printed.

Swan makes the physical card's number, CVC, expiry date, and the PIN available in Web Banking.
The **card number**, **CVC**, and **PIN** are considered sensitive data and must be masked by default.
The cardholder must complete a <Term id="consent">consent</Term> request before the numbers can be unmasked.

## Contactless payments {#contactless}

Contactless payments let users pay by tapping their physical card on the payment terminal instead of inserting it and entering a PIN.
To protect your users from fraud, physical cards issued by Swan have the following contactless limits.

| Limit type | Value | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single payment limit | €50 | Maximum amount for one contactless payment without using a PIN. |
| Cumulative amount limit | €150 | Maximum total amount spent in consecutive contactless transactions before a PIN is required. |

To reset the cumulative limit, the user must perform a chip-and-PIN transaction by inserting their physical card into a payment terminal and entering their PIN.

:::info Digital wallets
[Digital wallet payments](/cards/concepts/digital) (like Apple Pay or Google Pay) bypass these limits because they use [Strong Customer Authentication (SCA)](/users/concepts/consent/sca) through a password or biometrics.
These payments **don't count** toward physical card contactless limits.
:::
