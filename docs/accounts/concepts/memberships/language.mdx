# Membership language {#language}

You can choose and update the language used for <Term id="account-membership">account memberships</Term>.
The following communications use the account membership language:

- The **email** your account members receive inviting them to accept an account membership.
- The **letter** included with the account member's <Term id="cards-physical">physical card</Term>.
- When using their physical card, **payment terminals** and point of service (POS) screens.

By default, account memberships inherit the [same language as the account](/accounts/concepts/account/language).
But not all account members may prefer the language chosen by the <Term id="account-holder">account holder</Term>.

You can update the language for **each** account membership with the API.
If you use Swan's Web Banking interface, eligible account members can choose the preferred language when inviting new account members through the app.
Account members can also use the app to update their preferred language independently.

## Supported languages {#language-list}

Several languages are available for account memberships:

- Dutch (`nl`)
- English (`en`)
- Finnish (`fi`)
- French (`fr`)
- German (`de`)
- Italian (`it`)
- Portuguese (`pt`)
- Spanish (`es`)

## Physical cards and membership language {#language-cards}

The language used for physical cards **can't be updated**: like the four-digit PIN, it's coded on the card's chip.
This holds for renewed cards too, because the expiring card's chip is replicated for the new card.
