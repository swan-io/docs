# International Credit Transfers

<p className="ia-lede">Send and receive money almost anywhere in the world, in over 40 currencies, using local payment rails and the SWIFT network.</p>

## Overview {#overview}

With Swan International Credit Transfers, you can:

1. Exchange euros for a different currency, then transfer that money on either local rails or the SWIFT network.
1. Transfer euros outside of the eurozone using the SWIFT network.
1. Receive credit transfers initiated outside of the eurozone. You'll receive these transfers in euros as Swan doesn't store money in currency other than euros.

## Risk with international transfers {#risk}

International transfers cross borders, currencies, and jurisdictions, so Swan applies a risk-based approach: closer transaction review, both automated and manual, and [dynamic forms](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/international/outgoing#dynamic-forms) in the International Credit Transfer API.

## Fees {#fees}

There are fees for executing international transfers according to the non-euro currency selected.

Each eligible currency is **assigned to a fee group**, numbered 1 through 4.
Swan determined the groups based on how much it costs to execute a transfer with that currency.
For example, it costs less to execute a transfer between euros and a currency in fee group 1 than with a currency in fee group 3.

The fees listed on this page are always paid by the **Swan user**.

| Fee group | Outgoing transfer fee | Incoming transfer fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | €5 + 0.6% | €5 (+ 0.6%) |
| Group 2 | €5 + 1% | €5 (+ 1%) |
| Group 3 | €5 + 2% | €5 (+ 2%) |
| Group 4 | €5 + 5% | €5 (+ 5%) |

- **Outgoing transfers**: an **additional** €5 fee is charged when sending **USD**, **GBP**, and **EUR** through SWIFT. For these currencies, the total fee is €10 + the percentage indicated in the table.
- **Incoming transfers**: the additional fee percentage (in parentheses) only applies to transfers not already converted into euros.

**Fees are charged in their own transaction**.
Therefore, each International Credit Transfer **creates two transactions** on your transaction history: one for the transfer and one for fees.
Fees and transfer transactions are created, booked, and rejected simultaneously.

:::note Fees can vary
This section shares Swan's **standard pricing**, which might vary based on your contract.
:::

## Outgoing transfers {#outgoing}

Outgoing transfers go over local rails or SWIFT, exchange euros at a mid-market rate locked for one business day, and are capped at €100,000. The API collects beneficiary and transaction details through dynamic forms.
Local rails carry local currencies; the `swift_code` rail carries `USD`, `EUR`, and `GBP` only. Refer to [allowed countries, rails, and fee groups](/payments/reference/international-transfers).
Refer to [outgoing International Credit Transfers](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/international/outgoing) and their [status flow](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/statuses#international-outgoing), which never includes `Upcoming`.

## Incoming transfers {#incoming}

Any debtor can send money to a Swan account through SWIFT, with intermediary banks converting to euros and deducting their fees along the way. Swan books the remaining amount immediately.
Refer to [incoming International Credit Transfers](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/international/incoming).
