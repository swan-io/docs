# Change the account administrator

<p className="ia-lede">Change the <Term id="legal-representative">legal representative</Term> who administers a company's Swan accounts. Swan reviews the request, collects documents, and sets the new administrator as the legal representative on all of the <Term id="account-holder">account holder</Term>'s accounts.</p>

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

:::info
Account administrator changes are only available for **company** account holders. Individual accounts and self-employed account holders aren't eligible.
:::
