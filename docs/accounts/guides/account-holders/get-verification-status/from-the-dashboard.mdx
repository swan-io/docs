# Get account holder verification status from the Dashboard

:::caution Placeholder: to be completed

Review an <Term id="account-holder">account holder</Term>'s verification status, and any missing verification requirements, from your Dashboard.

## Steps

1. Go to **Dashboard** > **Data** > **Account holders**.
1. Filter the list by verification requirements if you're looking for account holders waiting on a specific requirement.
1. Open the account holder to see their verification status and, for `WaitingForInformation`, the missing [verification requirements](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/verification-statuses#waiting-for-info).
