# Billing

<p className="ia-lede">Understand how Swan charges banking fees, which fees you can customize, how usage-based metrics are billed, and when invoices are generated.</p>

Swan **[charges banking fees](/accounts/concepts/billing/fees)** directly from each Swan account in near real-time, as defined in the Terms and Conditions.
For each fee charged to the account, a transaction is created automatically and included on the monthly account statement.
Some features are billed by [usage-based metrics](/accounts/concepts/billing/usage-metrics) rather than fixed fees.

**Billing begins automatically** when an account is created, for both company and individual accounts.
For all accounts created before March 2023, the billing module was activated on 1 March 2023.

To help you and your users keep track of fees, Swan automatically [generates a dedicated invoice](/accounts/concepts/billing/invoices) for company <Term id="account-holder">account holders</Term> each monthly billing period in which banking fees were charged.
