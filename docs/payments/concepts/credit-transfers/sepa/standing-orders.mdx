# Standing Orders

<p className="ia-lede">A <Term id="standing-order">Standing Order</Term> is a scheduled, recurring <Term id="credit-transfer">credit transfer</Term> on the SEPA network. Consent is given once, then each occurrence sends a fixed amount or whatever sits above a target balance.</p>

import StandingOrderDefinition from '../../../../_shared/definitions/_standing-order.mdx';

> <StandingOrderDefinition />

Standing Orders are scheduled, recurring credit transfers that, at Swan, are sent on the SEPA network.
Schedule them on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis.
Standing Orders can be sent as SEPA Credit Transfers or Instant SEPA Credit Transfers, and [fallback](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/sepa/instant#instant-fallback) applies for Standing Orders sent instantly.

## Consent {#standing-orders-consent}

As with all credit transfers, setting up a Standing Order is a [sensitive operation](/users/reference/sensitive-operations).
After the initial <Term id="consent">consent</Term>, however, subsequent Standing Orders are sent as scheduled without further consent.

## Types of Standing Orders {#standing-orders}

Swan offers two types of Standing Orders:

| Type | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed amount** | Choose a specific amount to send to your beneficiary on a recurring basis. | Transfer €500 each month to cover a car payment. |
| **Target balance** | Choose how much money you'd like to remain in your account after the Standing Order is sent. Send the remaining balance to your beneficiary. | Transfer everything above €1,000 to another account at the end of each day. |

## Standing Order statuses {#standing-orders-statuses}

Standing Orders cycle through their own [Standing Order statuses](/payments/concepts/credit-transfers/statuses#statuses-standing-orders), which differ from other credit transfers.
