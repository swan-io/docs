## Issue cards end to end {#issue}

Use this overarching journey to help you issue the types of cards you need with the design you want.
Not every guide linked here applies to every use case.

### Step 1: Configure your card design {#issue-design}

Choose a standard or custom card design, then choose your packaging.

- [Configure standard cards](/cards/guides/design/standard)
- [Design custom cards](/cards/guides/design/custom)
- [Choose your packaging](/cards/concepts/design#packaging) (available for both standard and custom card designs)

### Step 2: Add account members {#issue-members}

Before being issued a card, each future cardholder must become a member of that account.

- [Add one account member](/accounts/guides/memberships/add-one)
- [Add multiple account members](/accounts/guides/memberships/add-multiple)

:::caution Card language
[Card language](/cards/concepts/card-products#language) is chosen when adding account memberships.
Ask your user to choose their card language, or choose it for them, before continuing to step 3.
:::

### Step 3: Add a virtual card {#issue-virtual}

You can add one or multiple virtual cards.
You can also have the physical card printed at the same time the virtual card is created.

- [Add one virtual card](/cards/guides/virtual/add#add-one)
- [Add multiple virtual cards](/cards/guides/virtual/add#add-multiple)
- [Print a physical card when adding a virtual card](/cards/guides/virtual/add#print-with-virtual)

### Step 4: Get card information {#issue-info}

As soon as you add a virtual card, it's available for eCommerce transactions.
You'll need the card number and other sensitive information to make those transactions.

- [View a virtual card's sensitive information](/cards/guides/virtual/view-info)

### Step 5: Digitize or print the virtual card {#issue-materialize}

You might want to add the virtual card to a digital wallet or print a physical card.

- [Print one physical card](/cards/guides/physical/print#print-one)
- [Print multiple physical cards with group delivery](/cards/guides/physical/print#print-group)
- [Add digital cards to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet](/cards/guides/digital/add)

### Step 6: Update card settings {#issue-settings}

You can update the settings for a [card product](/cards/concepts/card-products) at any time.
Settings apply to all card types: virtual, physical, and digital.

- [Update card settings](/cards/guides/lifecycle/update)
