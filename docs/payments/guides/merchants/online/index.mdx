# Accepting online payments

<p className="ia-lede">Accept online payments for your <Term id="merchants">merchants</Term>: request the payment methods they need, then collect with <Term id="payment-link">payment links</Term>, <Term id="sdd">SEPA Direct Debit</Term>, or <Term id="direct-debit">Internal Direct Debit</Term>.</p>

:::info Merchant profile
Before accepting online payments, each merchant needs a [merchant profile](/payments/concepts/merchants#profiles).
:::

## Payment links {#links}

Payment links redirect customers to a Swan-hosted payment page where they choose among the merchant's eligible enabled payment methods. Learn how [payment links](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#links) work, then [create one](./cards/create-link.mdx) to accept card payments.

## Merchant payments {#payments}

Every online payment is tracked in the shared [merchant payment object](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments), with its [statuses](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#payments-statuses) and balances.
