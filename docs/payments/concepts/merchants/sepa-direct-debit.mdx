## Direct debit payments {#payments}

Merchant direct debit payments follow the shared [merchant payment object](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments): one payment groups one or more <Term id="transactions">transactions</Term>, with [statuses](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#payments-statuses) and [balances](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#balances) common to all payment methods.

### Payment object statuses {#payments-statuses}

SEPA Direct Debit payments never use `Authorized`, and refunds and disputes cover the full amount. The [merchant statuses](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#payments-statuses-by-method) page has the machine and the table by method.

### Payment object balances {#payments-balances}

The balances used for SEPA Direct Debit payments are `availableToCancel`, `totalCanceled`, `totalCaptured`, `availableToRefund`, `totalRefunded`, and `totalDisputed`. For SEPA Direct Debit, `availableToCancel` is the pending amount that can still be [canceled](#canceling). The [merchant payments](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#balances) page defines each balance.

## Schemes: Core and B2B {#schemes}

Swan offers two SEPA Direct Debit schemes: `Core` and `B2B`.

The [SEPA Direct Debit scheme table](/payments/reference/schemes#sdd) compares eligible account types, refunds, returns, mandate requirements, and timelines for Core and B2B.

## SEPA Creditor Identifier (SCI) {#sci}

A SEPA Creditor Identifier (SCI) is the reference code that identifies a creditor on the SEPA Direct Debit scheme, so banks can route transactions to the right creditor.
Merchants using Swan's SCI share it; a merchant can also use their own (see [SCI options](#sci-options)).

SCIs are composed of several elements, including:

1. ISO two-letter country code
1. Two-digit checksum
1. Creditor business activity code
1. National identification feature

For example, `FR12ABC0123456789` is an SCI from France (`FR`) with the check number `12`, and the business activity code `ABC`, ending with the national identification feature that changes country to country (`0123456789`).

### SCI options {#sci-options}

Swan provides an SCI, and merchants can use their own instead; both meet SCI requirements.
The SCI decides the name shown: with Swan's SCI, **Swan** appears on the debtor's transaction history and in dispute exchanges between banks; with the merchant's own SCI, the **merchant's name** appears instead. The [statement display](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#statement-display-sdd) table summarizes both.

Merchants can **change their SCI** from Swan's to a personal SCI.
[Update the payment method](/payments/guides/merchants/online/sdd/update) by calling the `requestMerchantPaymentMethodsUpdate` mutation.

Regardless of the type of SCI, it's good practice to **include your merchant's name** in the `label` field for all SEPA Direct Debit transactions.

:::note Personal SCI
If a merchant in France wants their own SCI but doesn't have one, Swan can help them get it: open a [Partner Support Request](https://supportform.swan.io/).
:::

## Rolling reserve {#rolling-reserve}

[Rolling reserve](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#rolling-reserve) applies to SEPA Direct Debit. By default, the Core scheme's reserve is determined by a <Term id="merchant-profile">merchant profile</Term> risk assessment, and the B2B scheme holds 100% of the payment over 3 business days.

## Payment amount limit {#payment-limit}

Swan may apply a [payment amount limit](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#payment-limit) to a single SEPA Direct Debit (SDD) payment. If a payment exceeds this limit, the API returns a `ForbiddenRejection` error. To query the limit, use the `paymentAmountLimit` field at the `merchantPaymentMethod` level when you [request the payment method](/payments/guides/merchants/online/sdd/request).

## Canceling payments {#canceling}

Merchants can cancel SEPA Direct Debit payments using the [`cancelMerchantPayment`](https://api-reference.swan.io/mutations/cancel-merchant-payment/) mutation.
To understand if a payment is cancelable, check its `availableToCancel` balance.
Only the full amount can be canceled.

Payments can only be canceled before transactions are sent to the SEPA payment gateway, on the business day before execution:

- **SDD Core**: until approximately **11:00 Central European [Summer] Time (CET/CEST)**
- **SDD B2B**: until approximately **10:00 Central European [Summer] Time (CET/CEST)**

After the cutoff, the payment proceeds to settlement.

## Payment mandates {#mandates}

<Term id="payment-mandates">Payment mandates</Term> are mandatory for all SEPA Direct Debit transactions, and you must declare them to Swan.
Refer to [merchant payment mandates](/payments/concepts/merchants/mandates#sdd) for declaring mandates, the required information, and one-off mandates.
