# Received payment mandates

<p className="ia-lede">A received <Term id="payment-mandates">payment mandate</Term> is the debtor's formal authorization for a creditor to debit their Swan account. What the mandate requires from Swan and the debtor depends on whether it covers Core or B2B direct debits.</p>

Received payment mandates can be `OneOff`, valid for a single transaction, or `Recurrent`, for multiple transactions.

Creditors create mandates and debtors authorize them off the SEPA network. On the network, each mandate is identified by the creditor's [SEPA Creditor Identifier (SCI)](/payments/concepts/merchants/sepa-direct-debit#sci) and a Unique Mandate Reference (UMR); the pair is [unique across SEPA](/payments/concepts/merchants/mandates#sdd-declare).
Information about the received payment mandate is embedded in each SEPA Direct Debit instruction sent by the creditor bank to the debtor bank. 

Received payment mandates are stored in the API's `receivedDirectDebitMandate` object.

## Received mandate specifications {#mandates-specifications}

What the mandate requires depends on the scheme. For **Core** and **Internal Standard**, the debtor doesn't provide the mandate to Swan, Swan doesn't verify it against each instruction, and the debtor can get an authorized debit refunded, no questions asked, within the refund window. For **B2B** (SEPA and Internal), the debtor must provide the mandate to Swan, Swan must verify every instruction against it, and authorized debits aren't refundable.
The [received direct debit scheme table](/payments/reference/schemes#received) lists the windows and timelines.

## Received mandate statuses {#mandates-statuses}

Received payment mandates move through their own [received mandate statuses](/payments/concepts/direct-debit/statuses#mandates-statuses).
