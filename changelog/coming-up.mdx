  <summary>⚠️ **Upcoming breaking changes**</summary>

   ⚠️ The `cardContractId` field on [`DigitalCard`](https://api-reference.swan.io/interfaces/digital-card#) is **deprecated**. This field is renamed `cardId` and carries the same information.

    🏦 The `createCapitalDepositCase` mutation is **deprecated** in favor of `createCapitalDeposit`. Migrate before the end of 2026, as the `createCapitalDepositCase` will be removed in the first quarter of 2027. Check the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for details.

    📆 On **November 2, 2026**, the `reasonInfo` field on `AccountSuspendedStatus` will be **deprecated**. Migrate to `suspensionReasons`. Check the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for details.

    📆 From **November 2, 2026**, new beneficiary details will be required for International Credit Transfers in Swiss Francs (CHF), and from **November 30, 2026**, for Thai Baht (THB). Refer to the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for more information.
