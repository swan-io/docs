# Card payment countries

Where card payments made with Swan <Term id="cards">cards</Term> are allowed or blocked. <Term id="payments">Payments</Term> are only supported in countries on the allowed list; any attempt to pay in a blocked country is rejected.
<Term id="transactions">Transactions</Term> in allowed countries may still be rejected for other reasons, such as insufficient funds or [card restrictions](/payments/reference/card-rejection-reasons).
