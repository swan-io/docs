# Verification renewal (Re-KYC)

As required by financial regulations, Swan periodically verifies <Term id="account-holder">account holder</Term> information.
Verification renewal applies to all accounts held by the account holder.

It's a mandatory process that happens every 1, 3, or 5 years, or when Swan detects changes in an account holder's profile.

:::warning Account suspension
Users must confirm their account holder information within 90 days. If they don't, Swan automatically suspends their accounts and restricts all related <Term id="ibans">IBANs</Term>, cards, and payment capabilities until they complete verification.
:::

## Completing verification renewal in the portal {#verification-renewal-portal}

Swan offers a no-code, partner-branded portal to handle the verification renewal process. You don't need to integrate with an API.
Follow the [verification renewal guide](/accounts/guides/account-holders/verification-renewal) for the portal steps, your communication preferences, and how to lift a suspension.

After Swan validates the review, the account holder's [`VerificationRenewalStatus`](https://api-reference.swan.io/enums/verification-renewal-status/) changes to `Completed`. Verification renewal doesn't impact the account holder's banking verification status.

## Verification renewal timeline and communication {#verification-renewal-timeline}

Swan sends the initial form and all follow-ups based on your [communication preferences](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/notifications).

The 90-day period starts when Swan sends the account holder the first notification.
At the 90-day deadline, Swan suspends all of the account holder's accounts if they haven't completed verification renewal.
The [verification renewal guide](/accounts/guides/account-holders/verification-renewal#lift-suspension) covers how to lift the suspension.
