# Merchants

<p className="ia-lede"><Term id="merchants">Merchants</Term> are <Term id="account-holder">account holders</Term> who accept <Term id="payments">payments</Term> from their customers, by card online or in person, by <Term id="direct-debit">direct debit</Term>, or by French check. They collect through <Term id="payment-link">payment links</Term> or invoices.</p>

## Overview {#overview}

Merchants are account holders who want to accept payments by card (online and in person), <Term id="sdd">SEPA Direct Debit</Term>, Internal Direct Debit, or French checks. They can do this either through a payment link or an invoice.
This includes contractors, healthcare professionals, artisans, and business owners, among others.

With a <Term id="merchant-profile">merchant profile</Term> and at least one active <Term id="merchant-payment-method">merchant payment method</Term>, you can help your merchants accept payments with Swan.

:::info Pending review
Before accepting payments, you need to complete a risk review.
To get started, contact your dedicated PIM (Product Integration Manager), or use the Partner Support Request form on your Dashboard.
:::

### Managing risk {#risk}

Accepting payments from customers comes with risk, and Swan prioritizes a **risk-based approach**.

Swan reviews all requests for merchant profiles, profile updates, and payment methods. 
Both profiles and payment methods initially start with the status `PendingReview`.

For payment methods, Swan may change the status to `WaitingForInformation` when additional details are needed. 
In such cases, you'll receive these details within the `verificationRequirements` list. New information must be provided at the Merchant Profile level.

To manage potential risk, Swan can change the <Term id="rolling-reserve">rolling reserve</Term> and the payment amount limit. If suspicious activity is detected, Swan can also **change the status** of both **profiles and payment methods** to `Suspended` or `Rejected`.
Swan can also reinstate a profile or payment method by changing the status to `Enabled`.
Finally, if Swan assigns the status `Rejected` to a profile or payment method, the decision is final and can't be reversed. 
The user must submit a new request for the account holder, which Swan will review as a separate submission.

Subscribe to numerous <Term id="webhooks">webhooks</Term> to facilitate your merchant integrations.

## Merchant profiles {#profiles}

A merchant profile holds the business, risk, and support information Swan reviews before an account holder can accept payments. Every merchant needs at least one.
Refer to [merchant profiles](/payments/concepts/merchants/profiles) for the fields, statuses, and how updates are reviewed.

## Payment methods {#methods}

A merchant payment method is one way a merchant can be paid, attached to their profile and enabled after Swan's review. A profile can have several, and each approved update creates a new version.
Refer to [merchant payment methods](/payments/concepts/merchants/payment-methods) for the available methods, statuses, and versions.

## Account closing and suspension {#account-closing}

When a merchant's account moves to `Closing`, Swan rejects the account's merchant profiles and payment methods with the reason `AccountClosed`.
Swan also cancels the merchant's `Upcoming` incoming SEPA Direct Debit <Term id="transactions">transactions</Term> (Core and B2B) that haven't yet been sent to the payment gateway, as well as upcoming check transactions, and the corresponding merchant payments move to `Canceled`.
Transactions already handed over for settlement complete normally. Returns, refunds, and chargebacks are still `Booked` on the account while it's `Closing`.

On accounts in `Closing` or `Suspended` status, Swan rejects the creation of merchant profiles and payment methods, payment link initiation, merchant payments, and <Term id="payment-mandates">payment mandates</Term>. The rejection identifies the account status as the cause.

Learn more about the [account closure process](/accounts/concepts/closure/processes) and [account statuses](/accounts/concepts/account/statuses#statuses).
