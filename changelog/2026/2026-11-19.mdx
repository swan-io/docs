## New features

📚 Your operations team can now answer more day-to-day questions on its own with our [Partner Operations guides](/topics/operations/). They walk your team through common account and payment tasks, such as verifying accounts, identifying users, managing SEPA Direct Debit mandates, and handling card disputes and chargebacks. The newest guides show you how to [manage account closures](/topics/operations/guide-manage-account-closures/) and [troubleshoot declined card payments](/topics/operations/guide-troubleshoot-card-payments/), including when to raise a [Partner support request](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new).

To search these guides from your own AI tools, connect your tools to the [Swan Docs MCP server](/developers/tools/mcp-servers#documentation-server).

## API updates

### Upcoming breaking changes

📄 From **14 December 2026**, the account statement API will stop returning its deprecated fields, and statement generation will reject the old `statementType` input. If you read or generate account statements through the API, move to the replacement fields before this date. These changes apply to the [`accountStatement`](https://api-reference.swan.io/queries/account-statement/) and [`accountStatements`](https://api-reference.swan.io/queries/account-statements/) queries and to the [`generateAccountStatement`](https://api-reference.swan.io/mutations/generate-account-statement/) mutation:

- `fees` is replaced by `feeCredits` and `feeDebits`.
- `status` is replaced by the `statusInfo` object.
- `type` is replaced by `format`.
- The statement download `url`, previously in the `type` object, is now only in `statusInfo`.
- The `statementType` input of `generateAccountStatement` is replaced by `format`. Requests that still send `statementType` will be rejected.

🔐 From **31 December 2026**, Swan's consent and authentication pages will stop working in iframes and popups. If your app opens these pages in an iframe or a popup, open them in the user's current browser tab instead, then use the redirect URL to bring the user back to your app. Follow the guide to [integrate Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) in a web app](/topics/users/consent/guide-integrate-sca-web/).
