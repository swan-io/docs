# Troubleshoot card payments and declined transactions

Use this guide to identify why a payment made with a Swan card was declined, decide who needs to act, and know when to contact the merchant or Swan Support. It doesn’t cover accepting payments from cards issued by other providers. For issues accepting in-person card payments into a Swan account using Swan’s merchant features, see [Troubleshooting: in-person card payments](https://docs.swan.io).

For the technical payment lifecycle and rejection reasons, see [Card payments](https://docs.swan.io). To display transaction details and rejection reasons in your product, ask your development team to use Swan’s [Get transaction information](https://docs.swan.io) API guidance.

For user-facing guidance, see the relevant articles in [Share these articles with your users](#share-these-articles-with-your-users).

:::info Key takeaways

- Check whether the payment is recorded in the Dashboard.
- If the payment is recorded, check its status and rejection reason before taking further action.
- A declined card payment isn’t debited from the user’s account.
- Some issues can be resolved by the user, while others require the merchant or Swan.
- Raise a [Swan Support request](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new) in your Dashboard when the issue persists, affects multiple users, or requires Swan to investigate.

:::

---

## What a declined card payment means

A card payment can fail before Swan receives the authorization request or while Swan is authorizing it. If Swan records the transaction, check its status and rejection reason in the Dashboard.

A declined payment isn’t debited from the user’s account. This differs from:

- `Pending`, when an authorization has been granted but the payment hasn’t been debited yet.
- `Booked`, when the payment has been completed and debited.
- `Released`, when an authorization has ended without a debit and the held amount becomes available again.

If a transaction stays `Pending` or appears stuck instead of progressing as expected, use the lifecycle guidance in [Card payments](https://docs.swan.io) and raise a support request in your Dashboard when you need Swan to investigate. This guide focuses on declined payments.

This guide uses `Declined` to describe a card payment that wasn’t authorized.

### Who acts

| Situation | Who acts next |
| --- | --- |
| The payment isn’t recorded | Ask your user to check with the merchant. Raise a support request in your Dashboard if the merchant confirms that its side is working and the issue appears to be related to the Swan card. |
| The payment is recorded with a card or account issue | Check the account, membership, card status, and card settings based on the rejection reason. See [Step 3: Check the rejection reason](#step-3-check-the-rejection-reason) below for the checks to complete and when to ask your user to take action. |
| The payment is recorded with a merchant or authentication issue | See [Step 3: Check the rejection reason](#step-3-check-the-rejection-reason) below to determine whether your user needs to check their card details, complete 3-D Secure verification, retry the payment, or contact the merchant. |
| The rejection reason is `PartnerRefused` or `PartnerTechnicalErrorOccurred` | Ask your development team to check the partner’s Payment Control implementation and configured rules. Swan can’t see or explain partner-defined rules. |
| The rejection reason indicates a Swan technical error | Ask your user to retry the payment once. If it still fails, raise a support request in your Dashboard with the transaction details and checks already completed. |

## Troubleshoot a declined payment

### Step 1: Confirm the scope

Confirm that the issue concerns a user paying with a Swan-issued card.

If the issue concerns accepting card payments into a Swan account using Swan’s merchant features, see [Merchant card payments](https://docs.swan.io). These payments use different payment objects, statuses, and rejection reasons.

### Step 2: Find the transaction

1. Go to **Dashboard** > **Data** > **Accounts**.
1. Select the user’s account.
1. Open **Transactions**.
1. Find the payment attempt and open its details.
1. Check the transaction status and rejection reason.

If the payment isn’t listed:

1. Ask your user to confirm the date, amount, currency, merchant, and payment method.
1. Ask your user to check with the merchant whether the merchant received or rejected the authorization.
1. If the merchant confirms that their side is working and the issue appears to be related to the Swan card, raise a support request in your Dashboard with the merchant’s confirmation.

### Step 3: Check the rejection reason

Use the rejection reason to select the relevant path. Don’t infer the reason from the user’s description alone.

#### The account or membership isn’t eligible

Check that the account and account membership are active. If the account or membership status prevents payments, follow the relevant account or membership procedure instead of changing the card settings.

#### The card isn’t active, is suspended, or is permanently blocked

Check the actual card state in the Dashboard or in your partner product:

- If the card hasn’t been activated, ask the user to complete your card-activation flow. If your product doesn’t update the card state correctly, ask your development team to check the activation API implementation. See [Activate your physical card](https://support.swan.io).
- If the card is suspended, check whether the user or an authorized account representative can unsuspend it through your product’s card-management flow. Swan’s Web Banking may be available for some users, but it shouldn’t be the default instruction when your partner product manages cards through the API.
- If the card is permanently blocked, it can’t be reactivated. Ask the user to use another active card or follow your replacement-card process.
- If the card was blocked after incorrect PIN attempts, ask the user to follow [Unblock your card after three incorrect PIN attempts](https://support.swan.io). The user may need to wait 24 hours before starting the unblocking process.

For the technical implementation, see [Block and unblock physical cards](https://docs.swan.io). This reference covers both API actions and the user unblocking flow, so use the part that matches the action required.

:::caution Card state terminology

`CardSuspended` and `CardPermanentlyBlocked` are rejection reason codes, not card status values. Check the actual card state before advising the user to unsuspend or replace a card.

:::

#### The user doesn’t have enough available funds or has reached a limit

Check the user’s available balance and the applicable card or transaction limits.

Don’t assume that every limit-related decline uses `InsufficientFunds`. Use the rejection reason shown in the transaction details and check the current rejection reason reference before explaining the cause.

If the user has enough available funds but a configured limit has been reached, explain whether the user needs to wait, split the payment, or ask an authorized card manager to adjust the individual card setting within the permitted limit. Don’t change the global card product setup to resolve one user’s decline.

#### A card setting doesn’t allow the payment

Check the individual card’s usage settings, including:

- International payments.
- Payments in a non-main currency.
- eCommerce payments.
- Withdrawals.
- The transaction type attempted by the user.

The user or a member with the `CanManageCards` permission can adjust individual card parameters through your partner product. Swan’s Web Banking may be available when relevant. If your product shows inconsistent card settings, ask your development team to check the data returned by the API and fix the inconsistency in your interface.

Don’t change a global card product setting to resolve a single user’s decline. Only involve your development team in a product-wide configuration change when the change needs to apply to all cards using that card product.

#### The PIN or contactless verification wasn’t completed

Use the rejection reason to distinguish between an incorrect PIN, a missing PIN, and a contactless reset:

- For `PinInvalid`, ask the user to retry once with the correct PIN. The user shouldn’t keep retrying an unknown PIN because repeated incorrect attempts can block the physical card.
- For `PinRequired`, ask the user to insert the card and enter the PIN.
- For `PinRequiredForFurtherTransaction`, ask the user to insert the card and enter the correct PIN to restore contactless payments. This doesn’t mean the card is blocked after incorrect PIN attempts.
- For `InvalidPinAttemptsExceeded`, share [Unblock your card after three incorrect PIN attempts](https://support.swan.io).

For contactless limits, share [Spending limits](https://support.swan.io) with your user.

#### The card details are incorrect

Ask your user to check the card number, expiry date, and CVC entered with the merchant. Ask the user to retry once. If the details are correct and the payment still fails, ask the user to contact the merchant.

Raise a support request in your Dashboard only when the payment continues to fail and the merchant confirms that its side is working correctly.

#### 3-D Secure wasn’t completed

For an online payment, the merchant may need to start or complete a 3-D Secure flow.

Ask your user to:

1. Retry the payment.
1. Complete the 3-D Secure verification when prompted.
1. Contact the merchant if the verification step doesn’t appear or the payment is rejected again.

If the rejection reason indicates that the merchant needs to resubmit the authorization, the merchant needs to check its 3-D Secure implementation. Don’t assume that every 3-D Secure error is caused by the merchant.

Share [3-D Secure (3DS)](https://support.swan.io) with your user when they need user-facing guidance.

#### The digital card or wallet isn’t active

First, check the payment type and rejection reason in the transaction details.

If the payment used Apple Pay or Google Pay:

- Ask the user to confirm that the card is still present and active in their wallet.
- Share [Set up Apple Pay](https://support.swan.io) or [Set up Google Pay](https://support.swan.io) if they need to enroll the card again.
- Ask the user to retry the payment.

If the rejection reason indicates an issuer or network refusal, ask the user to check with the merchant and use another payment method if needed. Raise a support request in your Dashboard only when the issue persists after the merchant confirms that its side is working correctly.

#### A country restriction applies

:::caution Restricted countries

A country restriction can’t be removed by changing an individual card setting. Don’t ask the user to keep retrying the payment. See [Allowed and blocked countries for card payments](https://support.swan.io).

:::

#### Swan returned a technical error

Ask your user to retry the payment once. Check whether the same issue affects other users or cards. Record the transaction details and error information, then raise a support request in your Dashboard if the issue continues.

#### Payment Control refused the payment

:::info When to involve your development team

If the rejection reason is `PartnerRefused` or `PartnerTechnicalErrorOccurred`, ask your development team to check the partner’s Payment Control implementation and configured rules. Swan can’t see or explain partner-defined rules. This article doesn’t cover the technical investigation of those rules.

:::

### Step 4: Escalate persistent or widespread issues

Raise a support request in your Dashboard when:

- The rejection reason indicates a Swan technical error and retrying once hasn’t resolved the issue.
- The same issue affects multiple users or cards.
- The card, account, membership, and card settings are correct, but every payment attempt still fails.
- The transaction isn’t recorded, the merchant confirms that its side is working, and the issue appears to be related to the Swan card.
- The rejection reason is unfamiliar and the next action remains unclear after checking the relevant documentation.

## Contact Swan Support

Raise a [Swan Support request](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new) in your Dashboard and include the following information:

- Account ID.
- Account membership ID, when relevant.
- Card contract ID, or the masked card details requested by the support form.
- Transaction date and time.
- Amount and currency.
- Merchant name.
- Transaction status and rejection reason, if recorded in Swan.
- Whether the payment was in-store, online, made through 3-D Secure, or made through a digital wallet.
- The checks already completed.
- Screenshots or error messages with sensitive card details masked.

:::caution Confidentiality

Don’t include full card numbers, PINs, or other sensitive card credentials. Avoid opening duplicate requests for the same issue. See [Filling out a support request](https://support.swan.io).

:::
