<details>
  <summary>⚠️ **Upcoming breaking changes**</summary>

  <div>

    📆 From **November 30, 2026**, new beneficiary details will be required for International Credit Transfers in Thai Baht (THB). Refer to the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for more information.

    🔝 `fundingLimitSettings` and `fundingLimitSettingsChangeRequests`, along with their related mutation, queries, and simulation, are **deprecated** and will be removed at the end of **November 2026**. Refer to the [15 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/15-october-2026) for more information.

    💳 On **December 1, 2026**, `inAppProvisioningData` will refuse a Google Pay digital card queried without `googleWallet`, returning `BAD_USER_INPUT`. Migrate to Unified Push Provisioning before this date. Refer to the [5 November 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/5-november-2026#upcoming-breaking-changes) for more information.

    📄 From **December 14, 2026**, the deprecated `fees`, `status`, and `type` fields will be removed from account statements, and `generateAccountStatement` will reject the `statementType` input. Use `feeCredits` and `feeDebits` instead of `fees`, `statusInfo` instead of `status`, and `format` instead of `type` and `statementType`. Refer to the [19 November 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/19-november-2026#upcoming-breaking-changes) for more information.

    🔐 From **December 31, 2026**, Swan's consent and authentication pages will stop working in iframes and popups. Open them in the user's current browser tab and use the redirect URL to bring the user back to your app. Refer to the [19 November 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/19-november-2026#upcoming-breaking-changes) for more information.

    🏦 The `createCapitalDepositCase` mutation is **deprecated** in favor of `createCapitalDeposit`. Migrate before the end of 2026, as the `createCapitalDepositCase` will be removed in the first quarter of 2027. Check the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for details.

    ⚠️ The `cardContractId` field on [`DigitalCard`](https://api-reference.swan.io/interfaces/digital-card#) is **deprecated**. This field is renamed `cardId` and carries the same information.

  </div>
</details>
