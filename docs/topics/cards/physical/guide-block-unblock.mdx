# Block & unblock physical cards

:::caution Blocked for incorrect PIN attempts
If a card was blocked without request, it might be because the cardholder made too many [incorrect attempts to enter their PIN](./index.mdx#pin-incorrect-attempts).
:::

## Temporarily block a physical card {#block}

You can temporarily suspend a physical card with the [`suspendPhysicalCard` mutation](https://explorer.swan.io?query=bXV0YXRpb24gQmxvY2tQaHlzaWNhbENhcmQgewogIHN1c3BlbmRQaHlzaWNhbENhcmQoaW5wdXQ6IHsgY2FyZElkOiAiIiB9KSB7CiAgICAuLi4gb24gU3VzcGVuZFBoeXNpY2FsQ2FyZFN1Y2Nlc3NQYXlsb2FkIHsKICAgICAgX190eXBlbmFtZQogICAgfQogIH0KfQo%3D&tab=api).

## Unblock a physical card {#unblock}

To unblock a physical card with the [`resumePhysicalCard` mutation](https://explorer.swan.io?query=bXV0YXRpb24gVW5ibG9ja1BoeXNpY2FsQ2FyZCB7CiAgcmVzdW1lUGh5c2ljYWxDYXJkKGlucHV0OiB7IGNhcmRJZDogIiIsIGNvbnNlbnRSZWRpcmVjdFVybDogIiIgfSkgewogICAgLi4uIG9uIFJlc3VtZVBoeXNpY2FsQ2FyZFN1Y2Nlc3NQYXlsb2FkIHsKICAgICAgX190eXBlbmFtZQogICAgICBjb25zZW50IHsKICAgICAgICBjb25zZW50VXJsCiAgICAgIH0KICAgIH0KICB9Cn0K&tab=api), you must:

- Be signed in.
- Use a user access token in the name of the card's account member.

A `consentUrl` is returned inviting the user to start the Strong Customer Authentication with Swan.

If you **chose to block the card**, the card can be used immediately after you unblock it.

## Unblock after 3 incorrect PIN attempts {#unblock-pin}

If a card was blocked because the PIN was entered incorrectly 3 times, the **cardholder** must complete the process to resume the card, with one potential interaction from your side depending on your integration.

1. **Wait 24 hours** from the last incorrect PIN attempt.
1. You or the cardholder call the `resumePhysicalCard` mutation to start the unblocking process:
    - **Cardholder**: In Swan's Web Banking interface, clicking **Unblock** calls this mutation. Refer to Swan's [Support Center](https://support.swan.io/hc/en-150/articles/16351372133789-Unblock-your-card-after-three-incorrect-PIN-attempts) for more information. If you don't use Swan's Web Banking, consider designing your integration with a similar action.
    - **You**: If your integration doesn't allow the cardholder to do so, you need to [call the mutation](https://explorer.swan.io?query=bXV0YXRpb24gVW5ibG9ja1BoeXNpY2FsQ2FyZCB7CiAgcmVzdW1lUGh5c2ljYWxDYXJkKGlucHV0OiB7IGNhcmRJZDogIiIsIGNvbnNlbnRSZWRpcmVjdFVybDogIiIgfSkgewogICAgLi4uIG9uIFJlc3VtZVBoeXNpY2FsQ2FyZFN1Y2Nlc3NQYXlsb2FkIHsKICAgICAgX190eXBlbmFtZQogICAgICBjb25zZW50IHsKICAgICAgICBjb25zZW50VXJsCiAgICAgIH0KICAgIH0KICB9Cn0K&tab=api).
    :::caution Wait 24 hours
    If the `resumePhysicalCard` mutation is called before 24 hours pass, it returns an error.
    :::
3. The cardholder **consents** to the operation.
    - After receiving consent, the [physical card status](./index.mdx#statuses) returns from `Suspended` to its previous status. Resuming also re-activates the [renewal schedule](./index.mdx#renew-suspended): if the card is close to renewal (between the 10-week and 8-week checkpoints), it moves to `ToRenew`. If you resume after the 8-week checkpoint, the card returns to `Activated` and is `Canceled` when it expires.
1. The cardholder enters their **original PIN** at an ATM or a compatible payment terminal ([reminder: a card's PIN never changes](./index.mdx#pin)).

:::info View PIN
- You can display the cardholder's PIN [with the API](./guide-view-info.mdx#view-pin).
- If you use Swan's Web Banking interface, the [cardholder can view their PIN](https://support.swan.io/hc/en-150/articles/16423151195677-View-card-numbers-and-PIN#h_01HND9G193R7CQV275TSGZ59RC) themselves.
:::

### Finalizing card unblocking at an ATM or a compatible payment terminal {#unblock-pin-finalize}

A cardholder can unblock their card at either an ATM or a compatible payment terminal. The two paths have different requirements.

**At an ATM:** the cardholder inserts the card and enters their original PIN. They don't need to withdraw money.

**At a compatible payment terminal:** the cardholder inserts the card and enters their original PIN to complete a purchase. If the PIN is correct, the card is unblocked even if the purchase itself is rejected.

The success of this process depends on three factors:
- Whether withdrawals are enabled in the [card settings](../index.mdx#settings) (`withdrawal`).
- Whether the cardholder enters the correct PIN.
- Whether the transaction is successful.

Following this process, the card will either be unblocked or remain suspended.

| Withdrawals enabled | PIN entered | Transaction outcome | Rejection reason | Card status change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **True** | Correct | Accepted | None | Card is unblocked |
| **True** | Incorrect | Rejected | `PinInvalid` | Card remains suspended |
| **True** | Correct | Rejected | Any reason other than `PinInvalid` (for example, `InsufficientFunds`) | Card is unblocked |
| **False** | Correct | Rejected | `TransactionTypeNotAllowed` | Card is unblocked |
| **False** | Incorrect | Rejected | `PinInvalid` | Card remains suspended |


#### Special case: Pending unblocking after calling `resumePhysicalCard` {#unblock-pin-pending}

After the cardholder has consented to `resumePhysicalCard`, they must still complete the PIN-unblocking step at an ATM or a compatible payment terminal.

If they use the card at a payment terminal that doesn't support PIN resets before completing that step, the transaction may be rejected with `InvalidPinAttemptsExceeded`, regardless of whether the PIN is correct. The card may then be suspended again.
