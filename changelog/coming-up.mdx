<details>
  <summary>⚠️ **Upcoming breaking changes**</summary>

  <div>
  
   ⚠️ The `cardContractId` field on [`DigitalCard`](https://api-reference.swan.io/interfaces/digital-card#) is **deprecated**. This field is renamed `cardId` and carries the same information.

    🏦 The `createCapitalDepositCase` mutation is **deprecated** in favor of `createCapitalDeposit`. Migrate before the end of 2026, as the `createCapitalDepositCase` will be removed in the first quarter of 2027. Check the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for details.

    📆 On **November 2, 2026**, the `reasonInfo` field on `AccountSuspendedStatus` will be **deprecated**. Migrate to `suspensionReasons`. Check the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for details.

    📆 From **November 2, 2026**, new beneficiary details will be required for International Credit Transfers in Swiss Francs (CHF), and from **November 30, 2026**, for Thai Baht (THB). Refer to the [1 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/1-october-2026) for more information.

    🌍 From **November 12, 2026**, country fields (birth, address, ID document issuing, and company registration country) will no longer accept territory codes such as `REU`. Use the corresponding country code instead (`FRA`). Refer to the [15 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/15-october-2026) for more information.

    🐣 From **November 12, 2026**, onboardings will apply new birth date rules: account admins and legal representatives must be 18 or older, while ultimate beneficial owners can be any age. Refer to the [15 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/15-october-2026) for more information.

    🔝 `fundingLimitSettings` and `fundingLimitSettingsChangeRequests`, along with their related mutation, queries, and simulation, are **deprecated** and will be removed at the end of **November 2026**. Refer to the [15 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/15-october-2026) for more information.

    🔌 From **November 12, 2026**, the `extra.additional_information` field in AIS transaction responses returns the `originTransactionId` instead of the `paymentId`. Refer to the [15 October 2026 changelog entry](/changelog/15-october-2026) for more information.

  </div>
</details>
