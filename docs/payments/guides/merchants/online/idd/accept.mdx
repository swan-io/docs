## Step 2: Request the payment method {#step-2}

Before accepting <Term id="payments">payments</Term> with <Term id="direct-debit">Internal Direct Debit</Term>, you must request it so that Swan can review and validate the payment method for your use case.

→ [Request the Internal Direct Debit payment method](./request.mdx)

## Step 3: Declare payment mandates {#step-3}

Declare your <Term id="payment-mandates">payment mandates</Term> to Swan.

Mandates are mandatory for B2B, and optional but recommended for Standard.

→ [Declare a payment mandate](./declare.mdx)

## Step 4: Initiate transactions {#step-4}

Call the API to initiate Internal Direct Debit <Term id="transactions">transactions</Term> so <Term id="merchants">merchants</Term> can accept payments from their customers.

→ [Initiate an Internal Direct Debit transaction](./initiate.mdx)

## Step 5: Refund transactions {#step-5}

When required, issue a refund to the merchant's client.

→ [Refund an Internal Direct Debit transaction](./refund.mdx)
