Your support team might speak directly with fraudsters, not only with victims.
These callers can be aggressive and use strong emotional arguments.
For example, they mention a relative in danger, a medical emergency, or a threat to close their account.

However intense the conversation, **don't change your usual controls** and **don't share extra information**.
Your support team should:

- Stay calm and factual, and avoid confrontation.
- Say as little as possible.
- Not confirm or deny any information about the account, including the balance, transactions, or actions taken.
- Not share anything about controls, thresholds, alerts, or detection mechanisms.
- Not confirm that fraud is suspected.
- Send the case to Swan as quickly as possible.

Your support team doesn't need to decide alone whether they're speaking with a victim, a fraudster, or a user whose account is being used fraudulently.
**When in doubt, limit the conversation and send the case to Swan.**
