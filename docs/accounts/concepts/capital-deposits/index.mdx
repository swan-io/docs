# Capital deposits

<p className="ia-lede">A <Term id="capital-deposit">capital deposit</Term> collects a new company's share capital and produces the certificate it needs to register. Currently available in 🇫🇷 France.</p>

It's the first step of [company onboarding](/accounts/guides/onboarding/company): you onboard the future company and its shareholders, deposit the capital, and get the certificate before the company is legally registered; once registered, the company is onboarded as an <Term id="account-holder">account holder</Term>.

## How capital deposit fits {#how-it-fits}

A capital deposit produces the certificate a new company needs before it can be onboarded as an account holder and receive its Swan account.
