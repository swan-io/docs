## Improvements
🪝 You can now track digital card creation and status changes, including Apple Pay and Google Pay card provisioning, by subscribing to [`DigitalCard.Created` and `DigitalCard.Updated`](https://docs.swan.io/topics/cards/digital//#tracking-status) webhooks. 

Use the new [query `digitalCard(digitalCardId: ...)`](https://api-reference.swan.io/queries/digital-card) to retrieve the updated digital card. 

🇩🇪 [`taxIdentificationNumber`](https://docs.swan.io/topics/accounts/memberships/#country-req) is now optional when onboarding German account members. Tax identification numbers are no longer required to add, invite, or update memberships to accounts registered in Germany. 

## API updates

### Upcoming breaking changes

⚠️ The `cardContractId` field on [`DigitalCard`](https://api-reference.swan.io/interfaces/digital-card#) is **deprecated**. This field is renamed `cardId` and carries the same information.
