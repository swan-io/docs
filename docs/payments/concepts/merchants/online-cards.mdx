# Online cards for merchants

<p className="ia-lede">Accept online card payments as a <Term id="merchants">merchant</Term> through <Term id="payment-link">payment links</Term>: supported schemes, transaction types, settlement, rejection reasons, and disputes.</p>

## Overview {#about}

Cards are the most common way for customers worldwide to pay.
Swan supports most major card schemes, including Visa, Mastercard, and *Cartes Bancaires* (CB) in France.
American Express isn't supported.

Merchants can use payment links to accept <Term id="payments">payments</Term> from cardholders anywhere.
Typical implementations of payment links include:
- Adding a payment link to an invoice.
- Sharing a payment link with a QR code.
- Sending a payment link by email or SMS.

Collect rent, share invoices, and accept customer payments through a Swan merchant account.

In the API, online card acceptance is represented by `OnlineCard`-prefixed types, distinct from in-person card acceptance, which uses `InPersonCard`-prefixed types.

:::info Merchants only
This section **only applies to merchants** accepting card payments.
Visit the [card payments section](/payments/concepts/cards) for information about payments made with cards from a Swan account.
:::

## Merchant card payments {#payments}

Merchant card payments group one or more <Term id="transactions">transactions</Term> in the shared [merchant payment object](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#object), which is how Swan recommends matching orders, invoices, and sales to the correct payment.

### Card transaction types {#payments-transaction-types}

There are **four transaction types** for merchant card payments.

Authorizations aren't included in a transaction type.
Instead, they're represented at the payment level, visible through the payment object status `Authorized` and payment object balance `totalAuthorized`.

| Transaction type | Explanation | Impacted account balance |
|---|---|:---:|
| `CardInCredit` | Captured payment that first impacts the merchant's `Upcoming` balance, then their `Booked` balance. | `Upcoming`<br />`Booked` |
| `CardInCreditReversal` | Refunded payment that first impacts the merchant's `Pending` balance, then their `Booked` balance. | `Pending`<br />`Booked` |
| `CardInCreditChargeback` | Disputed payment that immediately impacts the merchant's `Booked` balance. | `Booked` |
| `CardInCreditChargebackReversal` | Disputed payment for which the dispute is overturned, impacting the merchant's `Booked` balance. | `Booked` |

:::info Balances
The impacted balances are Swan's standard [account balances](/accounts/concepts/account/balances), which aren't related to the [payment object balances](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#balances).
:::

### Payment object statuses {#payments-statuses}

Online card payments use the full [merchant payment machine](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#payments-statuses), including `Authorized`. For online cards, `Initiated` means the customer submitted their card details through the payment link but the payment isn't authorized yet; `Rejected` means the issuer or Swan denied the authorization; `Canceled` voids the authorization so the funds can't be captured. Refunds and disputes can cover some or all of the amount.

### Payment object balances {#payments-balances}

All [payment object balances](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#balances) apply to online card payments. Cancelations must occur before the amount is captured, and when capture is automatic, `totalCaptured` always equals `totalAuthorized`.

## Rolling reserve {#rolling-reserve}

[Rolling reserve](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#rolling-reserve) applies to online card payments: by default, 100% of the payment over 5 business days.

## Payment amount limit {#payment-limit}

Swan may apply a [payment amount limit](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#payment-limit) to a single online card payment. If a payment exceeds this limit, the API returns a `ForbiddenRejection` error. To query the limit, use the `paymentAmountLimit` field at the `merchantPaymentMethod` level when you [request the payment method](/payments/guides/merchants/online/cards/request).

## Settlement date and booked time {#settlement}

Merchant card transactions are booked the **business day after the transaction is captured** at **18:00** Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).

For example, transactions captured on Tuesday between 00:00 (midnight) and 23:59 UTC are booked on Wednesday, assuming both Tuesday and Wednesday are business days.

## R-transactions {#r-transactions}
For online cards, <Term id="r-transaction">R-transactions</Term> are rejections and chargebacks.


### Rejected {#rejected}

When a card payment is rejected, the cardholder's account is not charged.
The [online card rejection reasons](/payments/reference/merchant-rejection-reasons#online-cards) list each code and the resolution to share with your merchant.

### Chargebacks and disputes {#disputes}

Cardholders might create chargebacks, which opens a dispute.
Merchants can view the dispute automatically; the payment object status, the transaction status, and the merchant's balance all change to reflect the dispute.

To respond to disputes, ask your merchant to [submit a request](https://supportform.swan.io/) to the Swan Support team.
