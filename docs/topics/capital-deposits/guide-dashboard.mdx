# Manage a capital deposit case

Manage a capital deposit case from creation through funds release.

Your clients complete the process through your app. You monitor and manage the case through the Partner Dashboard, and your integration connects both experiences to the Swan API.

This guide explains the six stages shown in the Partner Dashboard, the actions available to you, and the actions your app needs to support for company administrators and shareholders.

For API mutations, document uploads, and API status fields, see the technical guides.

- [Create a capital deposit case](/topics/capital-deposits/france/guide-create-case-v2)
- [Upload required documents](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-upload-documents)
- [Capital deposits overview](/topics/capital-deposits/)

:::info General capital deposit information

Review the [sequence diagram](/topics/capital-deposits/#diagram) on the general capital deposits page to see the interactions in more detail. The page also includes information about statuses, shareholders, required documents, and cancellation.

:::

## Who does what?

| Actor | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **You, the integration partner** | Create and monitor the case, provide information through the API or Dashboard, and make required actions available in your app. |
| **Company administrator** | Complete the future company's onboarding, provide requested information, register the company, and obtain the KBIS. |
| **Shareholder** | Complete onboarding and verification, provide requested documents, and transfer their capital contribution. |
| **Swan** | Review the case, documents, shareholder verification, and company information. |
| **Notary** | Issue the capital share deposit certificate, review the KBIS, and transfer the capital to the company account. |

## Dashboard flow

The Partner Dashboard groups the capital deposit process into six stages:

| Stage | What happens | Main responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Case creation** | The case, company onboarding, shareholder onboardings, and initial documents are created or collected. | You, your app, and your clients |
| **2. Shareholder KYC & Funding** | Shareholders complete verification and transfer their capital contributions. | Shareholders, with support from your app |
| **3. Case review** | Swan reviews the case, documents, shareholder verification, and capital transfers. | Swan |
| **4. Notary certification** | The notary reviews the case and issues the capital share deposit certificate. | Notary |
| **5. Company registration and KBIS** | The company is registered and the KBIS is provided. | Company administrator and you |
| **6. Final review and funds release** | Swan completes the company verification and the notary transfers the capital to the company account. | Swan and notary |

Your app should make each required client action clear. Monitor the case through the Dashboard or API and show the relevant next step to the company administrator or shareholder.

:::caution Additional information may be requested

Swan may request additional information from a company administrator or shareholder during onboarding, verification, case review, or final company KYC.

Your app should surface these requests clearly and explain what the client needs to provide. The case cannot continue until the requested information has been submitted and accepted.

:::

## Step 1: Case creation

### What happens

Use the API to create a capital deposit case.

The case contains information about:

- The future company
- Company and individual shareholders
- Capital contribution amounts
- The future company onboarding
- Shareholder onboarding information
- Capital deposit documents

Creating the case starts the account creation process for the future company and its shareholders.

See [Create a capital deposit case](/topics/capital-deposits/france/guide-create-case-v2) for the required input fields, mutation example, success payload, and rejection payloads.

### What your integration needs to do

Your integration needs to:

- Create the capital deposit case.
- Provide onboarding links or onboarding actions to the company administrator and shareholders.
- Make the required company and shareholder information available to Swan.
- Upload the initial capital deposit documents.
- Monitor onboarding completion and case progress.

Document upload can take place in parallel with onboarding and verification. Upload each document as soon as it becomes available.

### What your clients do in your app

The company administrator needs to complete the future company's onboarding.

Each shareholder needs to complete their shareholder onboarding.

Your app should show:

- Which onboarding tasks are complete
- Which tasks are still pending
- Which company or shareholder needs to take action
- How to continue or resume an onboarding

### When this stage is complete

The case can move forward when:

- The company onboarding is complete.
- The required company documents have been uploaded.
- All shareholder onboardings are complete.

## Step 2: Shareholder KYC & Funding

### What happens

Each shareholder needs to complete the required verification and transfer their capital contribution to their dedicated Swan account.

The process includes:

- Identity verification
- Account-holder verification
- Proof of address collection for individual shareholders, where required
- Capital transfer from an account held in the shareholder's name

Shareholders complete these tasks through your app or the onboarding experience you provide. You monitor their progress through the Dashboard or API.

### Additional information during shareholder KYC

Swan may request additional information while reviewing a shareholder.

This can happen after the shareholder completes onboarding and identity verification. The request may concern:

- Identity information
- Address information
- Financial information
- Supporting documents
- Other information needed to complete verification

Your integration should:

- Monitor shareholder onboarding and account-holder verification status.
- Surface requests for additional information in your app.
- Tell the relevant shareholder what information or document they need to provide.
- Provide a way for the shareholder to submit the requested information.
- Continue monitoring the case until the request is resolved.

Do not assume that shareholder KYC is complete immediately after onboarding. A shareholder can complete onboarding and still require additional review before the case can move forward.

### Capital transfer requirements

Each shareholder needs to transfer their capital contribution from a bank account held in their own name to their dedicated Swan account.

Shareholders need their new Swan IBAN to make the transfer. Your integration should provide the IBAN and the relevant transfer instructions in your app.

The transfer needs to:

- Come from an account held in the shareholder's name.
- Use an accepted bank account.
- Match the shareholder's expected capital contribution.

A transfer from an unsupported account or a transfer for the wrong amount may be rejected or returned.

:::caution Cancellation after shareholder transfers

Once all shareholder capital transfers are complete, you can no longer cancel the capital deposit through the API.

Contact Swan if cancellation is still required.

:::

### When this stage is complete

The stage is complete when:

- All shareholders have completed the required verification.
- All required shareholder documents have been submitted.
- All shareholders have transferred their capital contributions.
- Swan has accepted the transfers and required information.

## Step 3: Case review

### What happens

Swan reviews the complete case, including:

- The future company's documents
- Shareholder information and verification
- Proof of address documents, where required
- Shareholder capital transfers
- The future company's business activity and legal structure

The case enters the review stage once the required onboarding, documents, verification, and transfers are available.

If Swan needs a correction or additional information, the Dashboard shows that action is needed and the case can return to an earlier stage.

### If Swan refuses a document

If a document does not meet Swan's requirements, the case shows `Action needed`.

The refused document is identified in the Documents section, together with the reason for the refusal.

Your integration should:

1. Show the request to the relevant company administrator or shareholder.
2. Explain why the document was refused.
3. Collect a corrected document through your app.
4. Upload the corrected document through the API, or replace it from the Partner Dashboard when that action is available.
5. Continue monitoring the case until Swan completes the next review.

A document correction does not complete the case by itself. Swan needs to review the replacement before the case can continue.

### If Swan requests other information

Swan may request information that is not linked to a specific document.

In that situation:

- The Dashboard shows `Action needed`.
- The request explains what information is needed.
- Your app should communicate the request to the relevant client.
- The client needs to provide the requested information through your supported experience.
- Your integration needs to submit or upload the information to Swan.

### If a capital transfer is returned

Swan may return a shareholder's capital transfer if it does not meet the transfer requirements.

The case returns to the shareholder funding stage. The shareholder needs to provide a new valid transfer before Swan can review the case again.

Your app should explain:

- Which shareholder needs to act
- Why a new transfer is required, when that information is available
- Which Swan account and amount to use
- How to monitor the replacement transfer

Once the required information and valid transfer are available, Swan reviews the case again.

### When this stage is complete

If the case is complete and all requirements are accepted, Swan sends it to the notary for certification.

## Step 4: Notary certification

### What happens

The notary reviews the case and issues the capital share deposit certificate.

The certificate uses the API document type:

`CapitalShareDepositCertificate`

The certificate is required before the company can complete its registration.

You can view the certificate in the capital deposits section of the Partner Dashboard. You can also retrieve it through the `capitalDepositCase` API query.

The notary process typically takes up to two business days.

### If the notary needs additional information

If the notary identifies a problem, the case can remain in the notary certification stage while the issue is resolved.

Swan communicates the required action through the supported partner communication channel. Once the issue is resolved, the notary continues processing the case.

Do not present a future notary escalation status as part of the current flow until that status is available in the public API.

### When this stage is complete

The stage is complete when:

- The notary has issued the capital share deposit certificate.
- The certificate is available in the case documents.
- The company administrator can use the certificate to register the company.

## Step 5: Company registration and KBIS

### What happens

The company administrator uses the capital share deposit certificate to register the company with the relevant French business registry.

Once the company is registered, the company administrator obtains the KBIS, which is the official company registration extract.

The company administrator provides the KBIS through your app, or you upload it through the Partner Dashboard or API.

Upload the KBIS with the document type:

`RegisterExtract`

See [Upload required documents](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-upload-documents) for the upload process.

### If the KBIS is refused

If Swan or the notary refuses the KBIS, the case needs a corrected register extract.

The case can move back from:

`WaitingForNotaryTransfer`

to:

`WaitingForRegisterExtract`

When this happens:

1. The Dashboard shows that action is needed.
2. The refused KBIS is identified in the Documents section.
3. Your app should tell the company administrator what needs to be corrected.
4. Upload the corrected KBIS through the API or Partner Dashboard.
5. Monitor the case until the replacement is accepted.

The capital deposit cannot proceed to funds release until the required register extract has been accepted.

## Step 6: Final review and funds release

### What happens

After the notary accepts the KBIS, the notary transfers the capital deposit to the company account.

At the same time, Swan completes the final verification of the newly incorporated company.

The Dashboard shows two sub-steps:

| Sub-step | Completed when | Completed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KYC verification | The company's account-holder verification is complete | Swan |
| Funds release | The notary's transfer is received in the company account | Notary |

The funds transfer and company verification take place in parallel.

### Company KYC during final review

Swan's final company verification includes:

1. Reviewing the KBIS uploaded in Step 5.
2. Retrieving the company's UBO declaration from the French registry.

The company account-holder status may show `WaitingForInformation` while Swan processes this information. This status does not always mean that action is required from you or your client.

There are three possible situations.

#### Swan is processing the KBIS

The Dashboard shows `In progress`.

No action is required. Swan is reviewing the KBIS uploaded in Step 5.

#### Swan is waiting for the UBO declaration

The UBO declaration may not be available from the French registry immediately after the company receives its KBIS.

The Dashboard shows `In progress`.

No action is required. Swan retries automatically while waiting for the declaration to become available.

#### Swan needs specific company information

If Swan identifies a specific need, the Dashboard shows `Action needed`.

The request may concern:

- Supporting company documents
- Tax identification information
- Legal representative details
- Other information about the organization

Your integration should show the request in your app and explain what the company administrator needs to provide.

Upload the requested documents through the Supporting documents section or through the supported API flow.

### Capital deposit completed does not always mean the company account is open

The capital deposit process and company account activation are related but separate outcomes.

A capital deposit can reach `Completed` when the notary's funds have arrived in the company account, while the company account is still inactive because the final company KYC review is not complete.

Before communicating that the account is open, check the company account status directly.

The company account is activated only after the company KYC review is complete.

### When the process is complete

The capital deposit process is complete when:

- The notary's capital transfer has arrived in the company account.
- Swan has completed the company's KYC review.
- The company account is active.

## Cancellation

You can cancel a capital deposit case through the API before all shareholder capital transfers are complete.

Once all shareholder transfers are complete, API cancellation is no longer available. Contact Swan if cancellation is still required.

When a case is canceled, the case shows the `Canceled` status. The case details remain available for reference.

See [Cancel a capital deposit](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-cancel) for the cancellation mutation and cancellation reason codes.
