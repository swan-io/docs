# Manage a capital deposit case

Use the Partner Dashboard to monitor and manage a capital deposit case from creation through funds release.

Your users complete their tasks through your partner experience. They don't use the Partner Dashboard. This guide explains what each Dashboard stage means, what actions are available to you, and what information you may need to request from your users.

For API implementation details, see:

- [Create a capital deposit case](/topics/capital-deposits/france/guide-create-case-v2)
- [Upload required documents](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-upload-documents)
- [Capital deposits overview](/topics/capital-deposits/)

## Who does what?

| Actor | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **You, the partner** | Monitor the case, review Dashboard requests, upload or replace documents, and follow up with your users. |
| **Account administrator** (admin) | Complete company onboarding, provide requested company information, register the company, and obtain the KBIS. |
| **Shareholder** | Complete onboarding and verification, provide requested information, and transfer their capital deposit. |
| **Swan** | Review the case, documents, shareholder verification, and company information. |
| **Notary** | Issue the capital share deposit certificate, review the KBIS, and transfer the capital to the company account. |

## Dashboard flow

The Partner Dashboard groups the capital deposit process into six stages:

| Stage | What happens | Main responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Case creation** | The case, company onboarding, shareholder onboardings, and initial documents are collected. | You, the account admin, and shareholders |
| **2. Shareholder KYC & Funding** | Shareholders complete verification and transfer their capital. | Shareholders |
| **3. Case review** | Swan reviews the case, documents, verification, and capital transfers. | Swan |
| **4. Notary certification** | The notary reviews the case and issues the capital share deposit certificate. | Notary |
| **5. Company registration and KBIS** | The company is registered and the KBIS (in France) is provided. | Account admin and partner |
| **6. Final review and funds release** | Swan completes the final company verification and the notary transfers the capital to the company account. | Swan and notary |

The Dashboard highlights the current stage and shows when action is required.

:::caution Additional information may be requested

Swan may request additional information from an account admin or shareholder during onboarding, verification, case review, or final company KYC.

When the Dashboard shows `Action needed`, review the request and communicate it to the relevant user through your partner experience.

:::

## Step 1: Case creation

### What happens in the Dashboard

The case appears in the capital deposits section of the Partner Dashboard.

The case includes:

- The future company
- Company and individual shareholders
- Capital deposit amounts
- Company onboarding
- Shareholder onboarding
- Required documents

The case creation stage includes the following activities:

- Company onboarding
- Company document collection
- Shareholder onboarding

These activities can take place in parallel.

### What you need to do

Monitor the case creation stage and make sure that:

- The account admin can complete company onboarding.
- Each shareholder can complete shareholder onboarding.
- The required company documents are collected.
- The Dashboard does not show any unresolved action.
- Your users know which task they need to complete next.

Upload documents as soon as they become available.

For the technical case-creation process, see [Create a capital deposit case](/topics/capital-deposits/france/guide-create-case-v2).

### What your users need to do

The account admin needs to complete the future company's onboarding.

Each shareholder needs to complete their shareholder onboarding.

Your partner experience should make it clear:

- Which onboarding tasks are complete
- Which tasks are still pending
- Which person needs to act
- How to continue or resume an onboarding

### Company documents

The documents collected during this stage can include:

- Draft articles of incorporation
- Lease agreement for the registered office
- Power of attorney
- Other documents requested for the company

See [Upload required documents](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-upload-documents) for the document requirements and upload process.

## Step 2: Shareholder KYC & Funding

### What happens in the Dashboard

This stage shows the progress of each shareholder.

The activities include:

- Identity verification
- KYC review
- Document collection
- Capital transfer

The Dashboard can show different progress states for different shareholders. Continue monitoring the case until all shareholders have completed the required tasks.

### What you need to do

Monitor each shareholder's progress and follow up when a task remains incomplete.

You may need to ask a shareholder to:

- Complete [identity verification](/topics/users/identifications/#levels-processes). For shareholder accounts, the identification level is PVID. 
- Provide proof of address.
- Provide another document or piece of information.
- Correct information submitted during onboarding.
- Transfer their capital.

Don't assume that shareholder KYC is complete immediately after onboarding. Swan may request additional information after a shareholder has completed onboarding and identity verification.

### Additional information during shareholder KYC

Swan may request additional information while reviewing a shareholder.

The request may concern:

- Identity information
- Address information
- Proof of address
- Financial information
- Supporting documents
- Other information needed to complete verification

### If a shareholder is `WaitingForInformation`

During shareholder KYC, the shareholder's account holder status may be `WaitingForInformation`.

The status includes a list of verification requirements. The list may contain one or more of:

- `FirstTransferRequired`
- `SupportingDocumentsRequired`
- `UboDetailsRequired`
- `LegalRepresentativeDetailsRequired`
- `OrganizationDetailsRequired`
- `TaxIdRequired`
- `Other`

Review the requirements to identify whether the shareholder or partner needs to act.

If `SupportingDocumentsRequired` appears, check the list of requested documents and ask the shareholder to provide them.

The shareholder's status remains `WaitingForInformation` until the required information, documents, or transfer have been provided and Swan has completed its review.

When the Dashboard shows that action is required:

1. Review the request.
2. Identify the relevant shareholder.
3. Communicate the request through your partner experience.
4. Collect the requested information or document.
5. Upload or submit the information through the supported process.
6. Continue monitoring the shareholder's progress.

### Shareholder onboarding and dedicated payment accounts

Each shareholder must complete their onboarding before Swan creates their dedicated payment account.

Once a shareholder's onboarding is finalized, Swan opens a **dedicated payment account** for that shareholder. These accounts are:

- **Limited in purpose and duration**: they can only be used to receive the shareholder's capital deposit funds.
- **Monitored**: Swan controls incoming transfers before forwarding the funds to the notary.
- **Automatically closed**: Swan closes the accounts when the capital deposit is completed.

Shareholders must transfer their share of the capital deposit to this dedicated payment account. These are the only accounts they should use to transfer their capital.

### Capital transfers

Each shareholder must transfer their share of the capital deposit themselves, from a bank account held in their own name to their dedicated Swan account.

The shareholder needs their new Swan account IBAN to make the transfer.

The transfer must:

- Come from an account held in the shareholder's name.
- Use a bank account accepted by Swan.
- Match the shareholder's expected capital deposit amount.

A transfer from an unsupported account or for the wrong amount may be rejected or returned.

### Transfer timing

The timing of the transfer depends on the shareholder's KYC flow:

- **During KYC verification**: if Swan requests a first transfer, the shareholder may already have transferred their share of the capital deposit. Their new Swan account IBAN is made available at that point.
- **During the funding stage**: if no first transfer is requested, the shareholder is prompted to transfer their share of the capital deposit once their IBAN is available.

Shareholders can use either of the following [transfer methods](/topics/payments/credit-transfers/sepa/):

- **Instant SEPA Credit Transfer**: funds arrive immediately.
- **SEPA Credit Transfer**: funds arrive according to standard interbank processing times.

Your partner experience should provide shareholders with:

- Their new Swan account IBAN.
- The expected capital deposit amount.
- The transfer instructions.
- The status of their transfer.

:::tip Recommended practice

Configure your partner experience to send each shareholder an automated email or notification when their new Swan account IBAN becomes available.

This should include:

- The shareholder's new Swan account IBAN.
- The expected capital deposit amount.
- The transfer instructions.
- A link or instructions for checking the transfer status.

This helps them complete their transfer without waiting for manual follow-up.

:::

### When this stage is complete

The Shareholder KYC & Funding stage is complete when:

- All shareholders have completed the required onboarding.
- All shareholders have completed the required verification.
- Required shareholder documents have been submitted.
- All shareholders have transferred their share of the capital.
- Swan has accepted the required information and transfers.

:::caution Cancellation after shareholder transfers

Once all shareholder capital transfers are complete, you can no longer cancel the capital deposit case through the API.

Contact Swan if you need to request cancellation after this point.

:::

## Step 3: Case review

### What happens

**Dashboard status:** `PendingInternalReview`

Swan reviews the complete case, including:

- The future company's documents
- Shareholder information and verification
- Proof of address documents, where required
- Shareholder capital transfers
- The future company's business activity and legal structure

This review typically takes around one business day. It may take longer if Swan requests corrections, additional information, or a new shareholder capital transfer.

The case can return to an earlier stage if Swan needs a correction or additional information. Once the requested information or correction has been provided, Swan reviews the case again.

If everything is in order, Swan validates the case and sends it to the notary.

### If the Dashboard shows `Action needed`

Review the case progress and **Documents** section to identify the required action.

The request may concern:

- A refused document
- Missing information
- Incorrect company information
- A returned capital transfer
- Additional information about a shareholder or the company

### If a document is refused

If a document does not meet Swan's requirements, the Dashboard shows `Action needed`.

The refused document appears in the Documents section with the reason for the refusal.

You need to:

1. Review the refusal reason.
2. Identify the account admin or shareholder who needs to act.
3. Ask that person to provide the corrected document.
4. Upload the replacement document from the Dashboard, when that action is available.
5. Continue monitoring the case until Swan completes the next review.

A document replacement doesn't complete the review by itself. Swan needs to review and accept the replacement before the case can continue.

Refer to [Upload required documents](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-upload-documents) for technical upload instructions.

### If Swan requests other information

Some requests are not linked to a specific document.

When this happens:

- The Dashboard shows `Action needed`.
- The request describes the information Swan needs.
- You need to communicate the request to the relevant user.
- The user needs to provide the requested information through your partner experience.
- You need to submit or upload the information through the supported process.

Once the required information has been provided, the case returns to Swan for another review.

### If a capital transfer is returned

Swan may return a shareholder's capital transfer if it doesn't meet the transfer requirements.

When this happens:

1. Review the case and transfer information in the Dashboard.
2. Identify the shareholder who needs to act.
3. Explain the required correction.
4. Ask the shareholder to make a new valid transfer.
5. Continue monitoring the case until the replacement transfer is received.

The case returns to `WaitingForInitialRequirements`, which corresponds to the Shareholder KYC & Funding stage, while the correction is completed.

Once the new valid transfer has been received, the case returns to Swan for review.

### When this stage is complete

The case moves to Notary certification when:

- Swan has accepted the company and shareholder information.
- Required documents have been accepted.
- Capital transfers meet the requirements.
- No additional information is outstanding.

Swan then validates the case and sends it to the notary.

## Step 4: Notary certification

### What happens

The notary reviews the case and issues the capital share deposit certificate.

The certificate is required before the company can be registered.

You can view and download the certificate from the capital deposits section of the Partner Dashboard.

### What you need to do

Monitor the Notary certification stage and make sure that the account admin knows when the certificate is available.

The account admin uses the certificate to register the company with the relevant French business registry.

### If the notary identifies a problem

If the notary identifies a missing document, incorrect information, or another issue, the case may remain in the Notary certification stage while the issue is resolved.

Review any action communicated by Swan and provide the requested information through the supported process.

Don't treat the case as ready for company registration until the certificate is available.

## Step 5: Company registration and KBIS

### What happens

The account admin uses the capital share deposit certificate to register the company with the relevant French business registry.

Once the company is registered, the account admin obtains the KBIS, which is the official company registration extract.

### What you need to do

Ask the account admin to provide the KBIS through your partner experience.

Upload the KBIS through the Partner Dashboard when that action is available.

You can also upload it through the technical document-upload process. See [Upload required documents](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-upload-documents).

### If the KBIS is refused

If Swan or the notary refuses the KBIS, the case needs a corrected register extract.

The case can return from:

```text
WaitingForNotaryTransfer
```

to:

```text
WaitingForRegisterExtract
```

When this happens:

1. The Dashboard shows that action is required.
2. The refused KBIS appears in the Documents section.
3. The refusal reason explains what needs to be corrected.
4. The account admin provides a corrected KBIS.
5. You upload the replacement through the Dashboard or supported upload process.
6. You continue monitoring the case until the replacement is accepted.

The case cannot proceed to funds release until the required register extract has been accepted.

## Step 6: Final review and funds release

### What happens

Once the KBIS has been accepted, the notary transfers the capital deposit to the company account.

At the same time, Swan completes the final verification of the newly incorporated company.

The Dashboard shows two sub-steps:

| Sub-step | Completed when | Completed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KYC verification | The company's account-holder verification is complete | Swan |
| Funds release | The notary's transfer is received in the company account | Notary |

The funds transfer and company verification can take place in parallel.

### Company KYC during final review

Swan's final company verification includes:

1. Reviewing the KBIS uploaded in Step 5.
2. Retrieving the company's UBO declaration from the French registry.

The company account holder status may show `WaitingForInformation` while Swan processes this information. This status doesn't always mean that action is required from you or your user.

There are three possible situations.

#### Swan is processing the KBIS

The Dashboard shows `In progress`.

No action is required. Swan is reviewing the KBIS uploaded in Step 5.

#### Swan is waiting for the UBO declaration

The UBO declaration may not be available from the French registry immediately after the company receives its KBIS.

The Dashboard shows `In progress`.

No action is required. Swan retries automatically while waiting for the declaration to become available.

#### Swan needs specific company information

If Swan identifies a specific need, the Dashboard shows `Action needed`.

The request may concern:

- Supporting company documents
- Tax identification information
- Legal representative details
- Other information about the organization

You need to communicate the request to the account admin and collect the requested information through your partner experience.

Upload the requested documents through the Dashboard or the supported document-upload process.

### A completed capital deposit does not always mean that the company account is active

The capital deposit case and the company account have separate statuses.

The capital deposit case reaches `Completed` when the notary's transfer arrives in the company account. This status doesn't confirm that the company account is active.

Swan may still need to complete the company's final KYC review.

Before telling the account admin that the account is open, check the company account status separately.

The company account is active only after the company KYC review is complete.

### When the process is complete

Check both outcomes separately:

- The capital deposit case shows `Completed`.
- The company account status shows that the account is active.

Your partner experience should communicate these outcomes separately so that the account admin knows whether the funds have arrived and whether the account is ready to use.

## Cancellation

You can cancel a capital deposit case before all shareholder capital transfers are complete.

Once all shareholder transfers are complete, API cancellation is no longer available. Contact Swan if cancellation is still required.

When a case is canceled, the Dashboard shows the `Canceled` status. The case details remain available for reference.

See [Cancel a capital deposit](/topics/capital-deposits/guide-cancel) for the cancellation process and reason codes.
