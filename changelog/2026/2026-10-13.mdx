## Improvements

🇫🇷 **Birth postal code only for users born in France**: Swan now asks for the birth postal code at sign-up only when the birth country is France. You no longer need to collect or prefill it for users born elsewhere. Account admins in no-code onboarding are asked for it only when both the account country and their birth country are France. Check the [individual](/topics/onboarding/individual/fields/#birth-information) and [company](/topics/onboarding/company/fields/#administrator-birth-information) onboarding fields pages for details.
