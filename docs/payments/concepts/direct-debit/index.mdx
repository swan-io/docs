# Direct debit

<p className="ia-lede">With a direct debit, a creditor pulls funds from a debtor's account under a mandate the debtor signed. Swan supports <Term id="sdd">SEPA Direct Debit</Term> (Core and B2B) and Swan <Term id="direct-debit">Internal Direct Debit</Term>, whether the Swan account is the one being debited or the one collecting.</p>

## Overview {#overview}

Direct debit is initiated by a creditor to take funds from a debtor's account.
Common creditors who use direct debit include utility companies and real estate rental agencies.

At Swan, direct debit works in two directions:
- Swan can [receive instructions for an outgoing direct debit](/payments/concepts/direct-debit/received-instructions), which is the focus of this section.
- Additionally, Swan can initiate incoming direct debit, either for [merchants to accept payments](/payments/guides/merchants/online/sdd) or to [fund your own account](/accounts/concepts/funding).

Swan supports several types of outgoing direct debit.
Note the acronyms; while the documentation doesn't use them, the acronyms are sometimes present in the API and used in written and verbal communication.

| Direct debit type | Description | Acronym |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEPA Direct Debit **Core** | Issued by companies to individual or company accounts in euros between accounts in the [Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA)](/payments/reference/sepa#sepa). | SDD Core |
| SEPA Direct Debit **B2B** | Issued by companies to company accounts in euros between accounts in [SEPA](/payments/reference/sepa#sepa). | SDD B2B |
| Swan Internal Direct Debit **Standard** | Issued by a Swan company account to individual or company accounts within the same Swan project. | IDD Standard |
| Swan Internal Direct Debit **B2B** | Issued by a Swan company account to company accounts within the same Swan project. | IDD B2B |

:::info Swan Internal Direct Debit
Some use cases require that direct debit <Term id="transactions">transactions</Term> occur between two Swan accounts in the same project.
For example, perhaps you want to receive fees directly from your user's Swan account.

For Internal Direct Debit, Swan is the creditor bank, the Clearing and Settlement Mechanism (CSM), and the debtor bank.
Internal Direct Debit doesn't pass through the SEPA network.
Instead, these transactions must follow the rules defined by France's *Code Monétaire et Financier*.
:::

### Specifications {#specifications}

The [received direct debit scheme table](/payments/reference/schemes#received) compares eligible account types, refunds, returns, mandate requirements, and timelines for SEPA Core and Internal Standard against B2B.

## Received payment mandates {#mandates}

Before a creditor can debit a Swan account, the debtor authorizes them through a received <Term id="payment-mandates">payment mandate</Term>, identified on the SEPA network by the creditor identifier and a unique mandate reference.
Refer to [received payment mandates](/payments/concepts/direct-debit/received-mandates) for the Core and B2B specifications and the mandate object.

## Received direct debit instructions {#received-instructions}

The creditor bank sends Swan an instruction before the debit date. Swan matches it to a mandate, creates an `Upcoming` transaction, and executes it at 06:00 CET on the requested date if the mandate, account, and balance all check out.
Refer to [received direct debit instructions](/payments/concepts/direct-debit/received-instructions) for new and known instructions and the execution checks.

## R-transactions {#r-transactions}

An instructed direct debit can be rejected, refunded, reversed, or canceled. Each is a new <Term id="r-transaction">R-transaction</Term> linked to the original, which keeps its own status.
Refer to [direct debit R-transactions](/payments/concepts/direct-debit/r-transactions) for the eligibility rules and time windows.
