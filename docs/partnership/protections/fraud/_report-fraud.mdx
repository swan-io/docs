If your users are victims of fraud with their Swan payment accounts, the fraud **must** be reported to Swan.

:::danger Critical
Reporting fraud to Swan is **urgent** and **mandatory**.
To report fraud from your backend, see [Create a fraud dispute case](/topics/dispute-cases/guide-create-fraud).
:::

Fraud is reported to Swan with a **fraud dispute case**, from any of these channels:

- **API**: your backend reports on behalf of a user.
- **Dashboard**: your operations team reports on behalf of a user with the **Fraud report** form.
- **Web Banking**: your users report fraud themselves with the **Fraud report** form. They open it from Web Banking or from a link you share.

See [Fraud dispute cases](/topics/dispute-cases/#fraud) for which transactions can be reported and how.

:::info Available from 7 December 2026
Fraud dispute cases are available from 7 December 2026. Until then, ask your users to report fraud with a [Swan Support request](https://supportform.swan.io/).
:::

Fraud dispute cases are only for completed transactions.
If your user suspects fraud but no transaction has gone through, ask them to submit a [Swan Support request](https://supportform.swan.io/). For example, someone else has access to their account.

You can also [alert Swan](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new) to suspected fraudulent activity.

Swan processes fraud reports based on criticality.
All fraud reports are analyzed, but they're not all accepted.
Swan responds as quickly as possible.
