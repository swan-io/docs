# Accounts concepts

<p className="ia-lede">The models behind Swan accounts: from onboarding and <Term id="account-holder">account holders</Term> through the account itself, its memberships, <Term id="ibans">IBANs</Term>, funding, billing, <Term id="supporting-documents">supporting documents</Term>, capital deposits, and closure.</p>

## The account model {#model}

Onboarding creates a verified account holder, who owns the Swan account. Everything else hangs off that account: who can use it, how money moves, how it scales, and how it closes.
