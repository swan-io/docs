# Look up users from the Dashboard

Review your project's users and their <Term id="identification">identification</Term> information from your Dashboard, without calling the API.

## Steps {#steps}

1. Go to **Dashboard** > **Data** > **Users**.

User and identification information for your project's users is available there.
For the identification model behind what you see, review [identification levels and processes](/users/concepts/identifications/levels-processes).
