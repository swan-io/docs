## Improvements

🪝 You can now [track digital card creation and status changes](/topics/cards/digital/guide-get-info), including Apple Pay and Google Pay card provisioning, by subscribing to [`DigitalCard.Created` and `DigitalCard.Updated`](/topics/cards/digital/#tracking-status) webhooks.

To retrieve the updated digital card, use the query [`digitalCard`](https://api-reference.swan.io/queries/digital-card/).

🇩🇪 [`taxIdentificationNumber`](/topics/accounts/memberships/#country-req) is now optional when onboarding German account members. Tax identification numbers are no longer required to add, invite, or update memberships to accounts registered in Germany.

## API updates

### Upcoming breaking changes

⚠️ The `cardContractId` field on [`DigitalCard`](https://api-reference.swan.io/interfaces/digital-card#) is **deprecated**. This field is renamed `cardId` and carries the same information.
