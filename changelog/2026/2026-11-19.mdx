## New features

📚 Your operations team can now answer more day-to-day questions on its own. Two new guides walk you through how to [manage account closures](/topics/operations/guide-manage-account-closures/) and [troubleshoot declined card payments](/topics/operations/guide-troubleshoot-card-payments/). Each one lists what to check first, what to tell your users, and when to raise a [Partner support request form](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new). Find them with the other guides in [Supporting your operations team](/topics/operations/).

You can also search these guides from your own AI tools. Connect them to the [Swan Docs MCP server](/developers/tools/mcp-servers#documentation-server).
