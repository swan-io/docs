# Merchant onboarding

To accept <Term id="payments">payments</Term>, you need to request a <Term id="merchant-profile">merchant profile</Term> and at least one <Term id="merchant-payment-method">merchant payment method</Term>.
Both must be `Enabled` before your <Term id="merchants">merchant</Term> can start accepting payments.

## Step 1: Request a merchant profile {#step-1}

Request a merchant profile for your account holder using the API.

→ [Request a merchant profile](./profiles/request.mdx)

:::tip Payment methods required
Your merchant profile will only be reviewed after you request at least one payment method.
:::

## Step 2: Request a merchant payment method {#step-2}

Choose the payment methods that fit your merchant's business model:

- [Cards](./online/cards/request.mdx)
- [SEPA Direct Debit](./online/sdd/request.mdx)
- [Internal Direct Debit](./online/idd/request.mdx)
- [In-person cards](./in-person/cards/request-method.mdx)
- [French checks](./in-person/checks/request.mdx)

## Step 3: Provide missing information {#step-3}

If Swan needs more details, the payment method moves to `WaitingForInformation` with the list of verification requirements and <Term id="supporting-documents">supporting documents</Term> to provide.
You provide them at the merchant profile level, so the same information serves every payment method.

→ [Provide missing information](./missing-information.mdx)

## Step 4: Test your integration {#step-4}

→ [Sandbox guides](/payments/guides/sandbox) for merchant profiles, payment methods, and each payment method's events.
