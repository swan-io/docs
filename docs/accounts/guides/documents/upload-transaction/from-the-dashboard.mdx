Upload <Term id="supporting-documents">supporting documents</Term> for a transaction from your Dashboard, in response to a document request.

## Steps

1. Go to **Dashboard** > **Data** > **Transactions**.
1. Open the transaction that needs documents.
1. Open the **Supporting documents** tab to see the associated document request.
1. Upload the requested documents.
