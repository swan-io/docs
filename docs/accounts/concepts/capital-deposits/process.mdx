# Capital deposit process

A <Term id="capital-deposit">capital deposit</Term> involves four parties:

- **You**, the partner driving the integration.
- **Your user**: the future company and its [shareholders](/accounts/concepts/capital-deposits/shareholders).
- **Swan**, through the Swan API.
- The **notary**.

Together, these parties complete a few main actions, from opening the case to wiring the funds to the new company.

Once shareholders have transferred their capital, Swan and the notary complete the deposit: Swan sends the documents and funds to the notary, the notary issues the capital deposit certificate, you upload the proof of registration, and the notary releases the funds to the company account.

The Dashboard shows the progress of each case: [Manage a capital deposit case](/accounts/guides/capital-deposits/deposit-capital) walks through each stage.

## Capital deposit case {#case}

The [`CapitalDepositCase`](https://api-reference.swan.io/objects/capital-deposit-case) API object is the spine of the process: it compiles everything for a single deposit.

- Details about the future company
- Company account information
- [Shareholder](/accounts/concepts/capital-deposits/shareholders) information
- [Capital deposit documents](#documents)

The case moves through several [statuses](/accounts/concepts/capital-deposits/statuses#case), and a [cancelation reason code](/accounts/reference/capital-deposits#cancelation-reason-codes) explains any cancelation.

## Required documents {#documents}

Processing a capital deposit requires several documents from shareholders and the future company, [uploaded with the API](/accounts/guides/capital-deposits/upload-documents). Document upload can take place in parallel with shareholder onboarding and <Term id="kyc">KYC</Term>, so documents can be uploaded as soon as they become available. A document that doesn't meet Swan's requirements is assigned the [status `Refused`](/accounts/concepts/capital-deposits/statuses#documents) and must be uploaded again; all [required documents are listed in the reference](/accounts/reference/capital-deposits#documents-list).

## Sequence diagram {#diagram}

The full choreography between you, the shareholders, Swan, and the notary, from creating the case to the notary transferring funds to the company account:

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
autonumber
participant SH as Shareholder
participant P as Partner (You)
participant S as Swan
participant N as Notary
    P ->> S: Creates capital deposit case
    P ->> S: Finalizes shareholder and company account onboarding
    SH ->> S: Completes identification
    P ->> S: Uploads required documents
    SH ->> S: Transfers capital to their new Swan account
    S ->> N: Sends documents and transfers funds
    N -->> S: Uploads capital share deposit certificate
    S ->> P: Provides certificate for download
    P -->> S: Uploads proof of registration
    S ->> N: Sends proof of registration and bank account details
    N -->> S: Transfers funds to Swan company account
```
