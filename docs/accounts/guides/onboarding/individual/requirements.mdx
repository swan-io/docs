# Individual onboarding requirements

Before creating an individual onboarding, review what Swan collects, the recommended <Term id="identification">identification</Term> levels, and the requirements for your account countries.

## Individual onboarding form {#onboarding-form}

Through the individual onboarding form, Swan collects the following information:

1. User's **email address**.
1. User's **country of residence** and **residence address**.
1. Basic details about the **user's employment** and **financial situation**. This information allows Swan to perform two essential tasks:
    * During onboarding, Swan completes an identity analysis (<Term id="kyc">Know Your Customer</Term>, or KYC).
    * For the lifetime of the account, Swan monitors for fraud.
1. User [**signs up**](/users/concepts/user/sign-up) for Swan.

## Identification recommendations {#identification-recommendations}

Swan must verify the identity of every user.
Swan supports multiple [identification processes](/users/concepts/identifications/levels-processes), and some require a [first transfer](/accounts/concepts/account-holders/first-transfer).
For individual onboarding, the following levels are recommended based on the account country.

| Account country | Recommended level<br/>for individuals | Other supported levels |
|---|---|---|
| 🇫🇷 France | **PVID** | Expert + first transfer<br/>QES |
| 🇧🇪 Belgium | **QES** | Expert + first transfer |
| 🇩🇪 Germany | **QES + first transfer** | none |
| 🇮🇹 Italy | **QES** | Expert + first transfer |
| 🇳🇱 Netherlands | **Expert** | QES<br/>PVID |
| 🇪🇸 Spain | **QES** | Expert + first transfer<br/>PVID + first transfer |

Refer to [identification recommendations by country](/users/reference/identity-methods/recommendations) for the full table and country-specific notes.

## Field requirements {#field-reqs}

Individual onboarding collects different fields per country (account and account administrator), with required/optional/conditional rules and API mapping tables. Those reference tables live in [Individual onboarding fields](/accounts/reference/onboarding/individual-onboarding-fields).
