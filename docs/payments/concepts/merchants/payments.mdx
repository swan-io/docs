# Merchant payments

<p className="ia-lede">The merchant payment object groups the <Term id="transactions">transactions</Term> of one <Term id="payments">payment</Term>, whatever the payment method, and carries the statuses, balances, <Term id="rolling-reserve">rolling reserve</Term>, and limits that apply to it.</p>

## Payment object {#object}

One payment can include several transactions, such as a capture and a refund, grouped in the **merchant payment object**.
Swan recommends using this object in your integration to help <Term id="merchants">merchants</Term> match orders, invoices, and sales to the correct payment.

The transaction types created for each payment method, and the account balances they impact, are described on each method's page: [online cards](/payments/concepts/merchants/online-cards#payments-transaction-types), [in-person cards](/payments/concepts/merchants/in-person-cards#transaction-types), and [French checks](/payments/concepts/merchants/french-checks#payments-transaction-types).

## Statuses {#statuses}

The payment object moves through `Initiated`, `Captured`, and their end states. The full machine, and which statuses each payment method uses, are on the [merchant statuses](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#payments-statuses) page.

## Balances {#balances}

The merchant payment object has **distinct balances** to follow a payment's lifecycle.
Use these balances to check what happened on a payment and what you can still do, such as cancel or refund.

| Payment object balance | Explanation |
|---|---|
| `totalAuthorized` | The total authorized amount for this merchant payment. |
| `availableToCancel` | The amount that can still be canceled: for cards, the authorized amount not yet captured. Check this balance before canceling; the per-method cutoff is in the [rules table](#rules). |
| `totalCanceled` | The total amount canceled for this merchant payment. |
| `availableToCapture` | The amount available for merchants to capture if the capture isn't automatic. |
| `totalCaptured` | The amount captured by the merchant. For card payments with automatic capture, `totalCaptured` is always equal to `totalAuthorized`. |
| `availableToRefund` | The amount the merchant can refund. Use this balance to know if a payment is eligible for a refund. |
| `totalRefunded` | The total amount refunded for this merchant payment. |
| `totalDisputed` | The total amount of this merchant payment disputed by the customer. |

Some balances behave differently by payment method; see the balances section of [SEPA Direct Debit](/payments/concepts/merchants/sepa-direct-debit#payments-balances) and [French checks](/payments/concepts/merchants/french-checks#payments-balances).

:::info Account balances
Payment object balances aren't related to Swan's standard [account balances](/accounts/concepts/account/balances), which the underlying transactions impact.
:::

## Rules by payment method {#rules}

The same payment object follows different operational rules depending on the payment method.

| Payment method | Rolling reserve (default) | Booked or settled | Cancel before | Refunds | Returns and disputes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Online cards](/payments/concepts/merchants/online-cards) | 100% over 5 business days | Business day after capture, 18:00 UTC | Capture | Some or all of the amount | Chargebacks open a dispute |
| [In-person cards](/payments/concepts/merchants/in-person-cards) | None by default | Settled by <Term id="credit-transfer">credit transfer</Term> within one business day | Capture | Not available through the API: merchants request them from Swan Support | Chargebacks open a dispute; act through Swan Support |
| [SEPA Direct Debit Core](/payments/concepts/merchants/sepa-direct-debit) | Determined by a <Term id="merchant-profile">merchant profile</Term> risk assessment | 20:00 CET/CEST on the settlement date | About 11:00 CET/CEST, the day before execution | Up to 8 calendar weeks after due date | Returns possible |
| [SEPA Direct Debit B2B](/payments/concepts/merchants/sepa-direct-debit) | 100% over 3 business days | 20:00 CET/CEST on the settlement date | About 10:00 CET/CEST, the day before execution | Not possible | Returns possible |
| [Internal Direct Debit Standard](/payments/concepts/merchants/internal-direct-debit) | Determined by a merchant profile risk assessment | 18:00 CET/CEST on the settlement date (D+1 if sent before 12:00) | 12:00 CET/CEST, one business day before execution | Up to 8 calendar weeks after due date | Returns possible |
| [Internal Direct Debit B2B](/payments/concepts/merchants/internal-direct-debit) | Not required | 18:00 CET/CEST on the settlement date (D+1 if sent before 12:00) | 12:00 CET/CEST, one business day before execution | Not possible | Not possible |
| [French checks](/payments/concepts/merchants/french-checks) | 100% over 10 interbank business days | 2 to 3 business days after arrival at Swan's check provider | Receipt by Swan's check provider | Not possible | Returns up to 10 business days after `Booked`, extendable to 90 calendar days |

## Rolling reserve {#rolling-reserve}

Swan applies [rolling reserve](/accounts/concepts/funding/rolling-reserve) to merchant transactions as a **percentage of the payment over a period of time** (example: "100% of the payment amount over 5 business days").
The amount and duration vary by payment method and merchant risk profile, and some methods don't require a rolling reserve at all; the defaults are in the [rules by payment method](#rules) table.
For the indicated period, the amount isn't part of the merchant's [available account balance](/accounts/concepts/account/balances), after which it's released to the merchant.

:::info Webhooks
The [merchant payment method webhooks](/build/using-api/webhooks#events-merchants-payment-methods) notify you when a method's rolling reserve value or payment amount limit changes.
The `TransactionRollingReserve.Updated` and `TransactionRollingReserve.Released` [transaction webhooks](/build/using-api/webhooks#events-transactions) notify you when an individual transaction's reserve is updated or released.
:::

## Payment amount limit {#payment-limit}

Swan may apply a **payment amount limit** to a single card, SEPA Direct Debit, or check payment.
If a payment **exceeds this limit**, the API returns a `ForbiddenRejection` error.

To query this limit, use the `paymentAmountLimit` field at the `merchantPaymentMethod` level in the API.

## Statement display {#statement-display}

How your merchant's name appears on the payer's bank statement depends on the payment method.

### Online card payments {#statement-display-cards}

For online card transactions, the payer's bank statement displays **`SW*[MerchantName]`**, where `MerchantName` is the merchant's name from their profile.

The content of the `label` field may also appear on the statement, depending on the payer's bank.
Choose the `label` value carefully, as the payer might see it.

### SEPA Direct Debit {#statement-display-sdd}

For SEPA Direct Debit, the name displayed on the debtor's statement depends on the **SEPA Creditor Identifier** (SCI) used.

| SCI option | Statement displays | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swan's SCI | **Swan** | Default option. |
| Merchant's own SCI | **Merchant's name** | Requires [updating the merchant payment method](/payments/guides/merchants/online/sdd/update). How to obtain a personal SCI is under [SCI options](/payments/concepts/merchants/sepa-direct-debit#sci-options). |
