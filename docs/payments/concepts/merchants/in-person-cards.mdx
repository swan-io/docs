# In-person cards for merchants

<p className="ia-lede">Turn a smartphone into a card terminal with Tap to Pay: terms of service, transaction types and settlement by <Term id="credit-transfer">credit transfer</Term>, limits, refunds, rejection reasons, and disputes.</p>

For many businesses, accepting card payments in person is a must-have.
Retail stores, health professionals, home care providers, taxi drivers, and contractors all need to accept card payments on the go.

With Swan's API, <Term id="merchants">merchants</Term> can turn their smartphone into a card terminal.
They accept <Term id="payments">payments</Term> through a terminal app.

## Merchant card payments {#merchant-card-payments}

In-person merchant card payments group one or more transactions in the shared [merchant payment object](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#object), which is how Swan recommends matching orders, invoices, and sales to the correct payment.

## Terms of service {#terms-of-service}

When your users request access to in-person card payments, they must accept the terms of service for the product.

Include a checkbox in your activation flow with the following recommended wording:

> By clicking "Next," you accept Swan's Terms and Conditions and [PARTNER NAME]'s partnership conditions.

Replace `[PARTNER NAME]` with your company name.

## Card transaction types {#transaction-types}

There are **four transaction types** for in-person merchant card payments.

:::info Statuses specific to in-person card payments
These statuses are specific to in-person card payments.
Other payment methods might behave differently.
:::

Authorizations aren't a transaction type.
Instead, they appear at the payment level through the payment object status `Authorized` and the balance `totalAuthorized`.

| Transaction type | Explanation | Possible statuses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CardInCredit` | Captured payment that will be settled through a separate credit transfer. | `Upcoming`, `Settled` |
| `CardInCreditReversal` | Refunded payment that will be settled through a separate credit transfer. | `Upcoming`, `Settled` |
| `CardInCreditChargeback` | Disputed payment that will be settled through a separate credit transfer. | `Upcoming`, `Settled` |
| `CardInCreditChargebackReversal` | Overturned dispute that will be settled through a separate credit transfer. | `Upcoming`, `Settled` |

### Settled status {#settled-status}

The `Settled` status means a credit transfer was `Booked` on the account to settle the merchant.
Individual card transactions don't affect the balance; instead, the credit transfer affects the `Booked` balance directly. The `Settled` status is final.

Every `Settled` transaction includes a `settlementTransactionId` field. Use it to identify the credit transfer that affects the balance.
Each settlement credit transfer also includes the list of settled transactions.

## Payment object statuses {#payment-statuses}

In-person card payments use the full [merchant payment machine](/payments/concepts/merchants/statuses#payments-statuses), including `Authorized`. For in-person cards, `Initiated` means the payment was created on the terminal app and is ready to be taken; `Rejected` means the issuer or Swan denied the authorization; `Canceled` voids the authorization so the funds can't be captured. Refunds and disputes can cover some or all of the amount.

## Payment object balances {#payment-balances}

All [payment object balances](/payments/concepts/merchants/payments#balances) apply to in-person card payments. Check `availableToCancel` before canceling: cancelations must occur before the amount is captured. When capture is automatic, `totalCaptured` always equals `totalAuthorized`. Check `availableToRefund` to know if a refund is possible.

## Rolling reserve {#rolling-reserve}

In-person card payments are **not subject to a <Term id="rolling-reserve">rolling reserve</Term>** by default.
This could change if Swan's monitoring systems detect suspicious patterns.

## Payment amount limits {#payment-limit}

The minimum amount to create a payment intent is **€0.50**.

In-person card payments are **not subject to a maximum payment amount limit** by default.
This could change if Swan's monitoring systems detect suspicious patterns.

## Settlement process {#settlement}

For in-person card payments, the settlement is a credit transfer.

1. When the merchant payment is created, underlying transactions are created as `Upcoming`.
1. When the settlement arrives as a credit transfer, the transactions are updated to `Settled`.
1. The credit transfer is immediately `Booked`. It affects the balance and makes the corresponding funds available. Card transactions don't affect any account balances.
1. Transactions captured on a given day are `Settled` within **one business day**. No rolling reserve applies to the settlement credit transfer.

:::info Delayed settlements
In rare cases, a settlement can be delayed.
If it doesn't arrive within **5 business days**, contact Swan through the [Partner Support Request form](https://supportform.swan.io/).
:::

The minimum amount for a settlement credit transfer is **€1**.

## R-transactions {#r-transactions}

For in-person cards, <Term id="r-transaction">R-transactions</Term> are rejections and chargebacks.

### Rejected {#rejected}

When a card payment is rejected, the cardholder's account is not charged.
The [in-person card rejection reasons](/payments/reference/merchant-rejection-reasons#in-person-cards) list each code and the next step to share with your merchant.
