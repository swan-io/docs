### Card transactions {#transactions}

Each card `transaction` object contains specific payment data in the [`CardTransaction` interface](https://api-reference.swan.io/objects/card-transaction/), including:

- **Terminal ID**: unique identifier of the merchant's terminal in the Mastercard network
- **Merchant ID**: unique identifier of the merchant in the Mastercard network
- Merchant **name**
- Merchant **city**
- Merchant **postal code**
- Merchant Category Code (**MCC**)
- Original **amount** and **currency**

### Transaction categories {#categories}

Card transactions are categorized based on where and how the payment was made.
The `CardTransactionCategory` enum provides the following values:

- **InStore**: Point-of-sale terminal transactions made at a physical merchant location.
- **eCommerce**: Online transactions without 3-D Secure <Term id="consent">consent</Term>.
- **eCommerceWith3DS**: Online transactions with 3-D Secure consent.
- **Withdrawal**: ATM withdrawals.
- **Other**: Transactions that don't fit the above categories, including non-standard merchant flows and technical retries.

### Card transaction statuses {#statuses}

Card transactions move through their own [card transaction statuses](/payments/concepts/cards/statuses).

## Rejected card transactions {#rejected-card-transactions}

When a card payment is rejected, the account isn't charged. The `reason` in the rejected transaction's `statusInfo` explains why. Refer to the [card rejection reasons](/payments/reference/card-rejection-reasons) reference for the common codes and resolutions to share with your users.

## Contactless payments {#contactless}

Contactless payments let users pay by tapping their physical card on the payment terminal instead of inserting it and entering a PIN.

### Physical card contactless limits {#contactless-limits}

To protect your users from fraud, physical cards have limits when used for contactless payments.
These are the contactless payment limits for physical cards issued by Swan.

| Limit type | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Single payment limit   | €50   | Maximum amount for one contactless payment without using a PIN.|
| Cumulative amount limit | €150  | Maximum total amount spent in consecutive contactless transactions before a PIN is required. |

:::info Account balances
[Digital wallet payments](/cards/concepts/digital) (like Apple Pay or Google Pay) bypass these limits because they use [Strong Customer Authentication (SCA)](/users/concepts/consent/sca) through a password or biometrics. 
These payments **don't count** toward physical card contactless limits.
:::

### Resetting the contactless payment limit {#contactless-reset}

To reset the cumulative spending (€150) limit, your user must perform a chip-and-PIN transaction by inserting their physical card into a payment terminal and entering their PIN.

## Fraud and card transactions {#fraud}

When a cardholder is a victim of fraud, they must declare it to Swan.
If, after an internal investigation, the chargeback is admissible, Swan creates a new `InternalCreditTransferIn` credit transaction with the [status `Booked`](/payments/concepts/cards/statuses#statuses), which impacts the [account's booked balance](/accounts/concepts/account/balances).

The chargeback transaction is linked to the same payment as the initial card transaction.

## International card transactions {#international}

In order for your cardholders to make international card transactions, configure the [card settings](/cards/reference/card-settings#settings) to allow them.
For a smoother user experience, both `international` and `nonMainCurrencyTransactions` should be activated.

Unless your Terms and Conditions state differently, a **2% fee** applies to international card transactions.
[Mastercard's exchange rate](https://www.mastercard.com/content/mastercardcom/global/en/personal/get-support/convert-currency.html) applies to card transactions outside of [SEPA](/payments/reference/sepa#sepa).

## Processing card payments {#processing}

Card payments are first [authorized, then cleared](/payments/concepts/cards/authorization-and-clearing): the authorization reserves funds as a `Pending` transaction, and the clearing debits them a few days later, with several capture types and merchant flows. Online payments may also require [3-D Secure](/payments/concepts/cards/3ds) authentication before the authorization is accepted. After the transaction is created, Swan adds [enriched transaction information](/payments/concepts/cards/enriched-info) about the merchant.
