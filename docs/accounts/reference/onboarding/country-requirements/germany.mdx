# 🇩🇪 Germany

Registration documents and legal forms Swan accepts for <Term id="account-holder">account holders</Term> registered in Germany.

Mandatory documents (<Yes /> in the **Mandatory** column) are required to verify an account holder, regardless of how they're retrieved. All other documents may be requested on a case-by-case basis.

## Companies {#companies}

### Legal forms {#company-types}

| Legal form (German) | Description |
|---|---|
| Offene Handelsgesellschaft (oHG) | General partnership |
| Kommanditgesellschaft (KG) | Limited partnership |
| Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung (GmbH) | Limited Liability Company |
| Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung & Compagnie Kommanditgesellschaft (GmbH & Co. KG) | Limited Partnership with a Limited Liability Company as General Partner |
| Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung & Compagnie offene Handelsgesellschaft (GmbH & Co. oHG) | General Partnership with a Limited Liability Company as General Partner |
| Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt) (UG) | Entrepreneurial Company (limited liability) |
| Aktiengesellschaft (AG) | Joint-stock Company |
| Societas Europaea (SE) | European Company |
| Europäische wirtschaftliche Interessenvereinigung (EWIV) | European Economic Interest Grouping |
| Societas Cooperativa Europaea (SCE) | European Cooperative Society |
| Genossenschaft | Cooperative Society |
| Gesellschaft bürgerlichen Rechts (eGbR) | Registered Civil Law Partnership |
| Eingetragener Kaufmann | Registered merchant |

### Accepted documents {#company-documents}

| Document type (German) | Description | Details | Mandatory |
|---|---|---| :---: |
| Nachweis der Firmenregistrierung | Proof of Company Registration | <ul><li>Must be issued by the Handelsregister/Transparenzregister.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Gesellschaftsvertrag / Satzungen | Articles of Association | <ul><li>Must be stamped and signed by a notary</li><li>Or uploaded from the official source.</li><li>Must be the most recently published version, or dated within the last **6 months** if amendments have been made.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| UBO-Zertifikat | Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBO) certificate | <ul><li>Must include all direct and indirect UBOs (ownership ≥25%).</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Personalausweis der wirtschaftlich Berechtigten (UBOs) | Identity documents of UBOs | <ul><li>Valid identity documents in color</li><li>Both sides if applicable.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Vollmacht <br /> Personalausweis | Power of Attorney and ID of the legal representative | <ul><li>Required if the legal representative is not the account holder.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Formular für politisch exponierte Personen (PEP) | Politically Exposed Person (PEP) form | <ul><li>Swan sends this form to the end user (based on your communication preferences).</li><li>Must be dated and signed.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Finanzberichte (z.B. Steuererklärung) | Financial statements | <ul><li>Most recent annual report or balance sheet.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Eidesstattliche Erklärung | Sworn statement | <ul><li>Must be signed and dated within the last 3 months.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Organigramm des Unternehmens | Company structure chart | <ul><li>An internal company document showing the holding structure.</li></ul> | <No /> |

## Self-employed individuals and freelancers {#self-employed}

### Legal forms {#self-employed-types}

| Legal form (German) | Description |
|---|---|
| Freiberufler | Liberal professionals or freelancers |
| Selbständig/Gewerbetreibende | Self-employed in trade or business |
| Partnerschaft | Partnership |
| Gesellschaft bürgerlichen Rechts (GbR) | Civil law partnership |

### Accepted documents {#self-employed-documents}

| Document type (German) | Description | Details | Mandatory |
|---|---|---| :---: |
| Gewerbeschein | Business or trade license | <ul><li>Required for self-employed in trade or business</li><li>Also for GbR, if available</li><li>Must be issued by local authorities and dated.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Berufsanmeldung or Berufszulassung | Professional registration or professional license | <ul><li>Required for regulated liberal professionals or freelancers.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Freiberufler-registrierung | Freelancer registration | <ul><li>Required for liberal professionals or freelancers.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Satzung der Gesellschaft | Articles of Association | <ul><li>For Partnerships (Partnerschaft).</li><li>Must be dated and signed.</li><li>Must be the most recently published version.</li><li>If amendments have been made, it must be dated within the last **6 months**.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Bescheinigung über die steuerliche Erfassung | Tax registration certificate | <ul><li>Required for liberal professionals or freelancers</li><li>Issued by the Finanzamt.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Brief vom Finanzamt | Letter from the tax office | <ul><li>Letter confirming tax ID or VAT number.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Steuerbescheid | Tax declaration (most recent) | <ul><li>Must be issued by the tax office and dated within the last year.</li></ul> | <No /> |

## Associations {#associations}

### Legal forms {#association-types}

| Legal form (German) | Description |
|---|---|
| Eingetragener Verein (e.V.) | Registered association |
| Nicht eingetragener Verein | Unregistered association |
| Gemeinnütziger Verein | Charitable association |
| Stiftung | Foundation |

### Accepted documents {#association-documents}

| Document type (German) | Description | Details | Mandatory |
|---|---|---| :---: |
| Vereinsregisterauszug / VR-Auszug | Proof of registration | <ul><li>Must be issued by the *Vereinsregister* (association register).</li><li>Must be the latest available extract, with no dissolution or liquidation flags.</li><li>Retrieved by Swan when available; otherwise provided by the account holder.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Satzung des Vereins / Vereinssatzung | Articles of Association (statutes) | <ul><li>Must be dated and signed.</li><li>Must be the most recently published version.</li><li>If amendments have been made, it must be dated within the last **6 months**.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Gründungsprotokoll / Errichtungsurkunde | Founding deed | <ul><li>Required when founding documents aren't included in the statutes or register extract.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Protokoll der Mitgliederversammlung | General meeting minutes | <ul><li>Required when governance or representation powers aren't clear from the register extract.</li><li>Must be the latest version, showing names, roles, effective mandate date, and signatures.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Vorstandswahlprotokoll | Board or president appointment decision | <ul><li>Required when the register extract doesn't clearly show current board members or representation powers.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Organisationsstruktur / Organigramm | Governance structure documentation | <ul><li>Required when the association's governance structure is complex or not clear from other documents.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Mitgliederliste / Aufnahmeordnung | Membership list or admission rules | <ul><li>Provided by the account holder.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Personalausweis / Reisepass des Kontoverwalters | Account administrator identity document | <ul><li>Must be a valid identity document in color.</li><li>Both sides, if applicable.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Identitätsprüfung des Kontoverwalters | Account administrator identity verification | <ul><li>Provided via eID, PostIdent, VideoIdent, or expert verification.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Personalausweis des gesetzlichen Vertreters | Identity document of the legal representative | <ul><li>Must be a valid identity document in color.</li><li>Both sides, if applicable.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Vertretungsbescheinigung / Vereinsregisterauszug | Legal representative authority | <ul><li>Must confirm representation powers granted to the legal representative.</li><li>Must be dated.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Vollmacht zur Kontoeröffnung und -verwaltung | Power of Attorney | <ul><li>Required when the person opening the account is not a legal board member (*Vorstand*).</li><li>Must clearly grant account-opening and management powers.</li><li>Must be signed by the correct legal representative(s) and dated within the last year.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Selbstauskunft zum wirtschaftlich Berechtigten / Transparenzregisterauszug | UBO declaration (transparency register extract) | <ul><li>Provided by the account holder.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Mittelherkunftsnachweis | Source of funds evidence | <ul><li>Provided by the account holder.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Aktivitäts- und Finanzierungsfragebogen | Activity and funding questionnaire | <ul><li>Provided by the account holder.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Finanzberichte / Jahresabschluss / Tätigkeitsbericht | Annual accounts, financial statements, or evidence of financial activity | <ul><li>Most recent annual report or balance sheet.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Kontoauszüge | Bank statements | <ul><li>3-6 months of statements.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Spender- / Begünstigtenregister | Donor or beneficiary registers | <ul><li>Provided by the account holder.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Formular für politisch exponierte Personen (PEP) | Politically Exposed Person (PEP) form | <ul><li>Swan sends this form to the end user (based on your communication preferences).</li><li>Must be dated and signed.</li></ul> | <No /> |

## Homeowners associations {#homeowners-associations}

### Legal forms {#hoa-types}

| Legal form (German) | Description |
|---|---|
| Wohnungseigentümergemeinschaft (WEG) | Homeowners association |

### Accepted documents {#hoa-documents}

| Document type (German) | Description | Details | Mandatory |
|---|---|---| :---: |
| Grundbuchauszug | Proof of registration | <ul><li>Official registration document showing the homeowners association is active.</li><li>Must be dated.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Teilungserklärung | Declaration of division | <ul><li>The founding document of the homeowners association.</li><li>Must be the most recently published version.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Protokoll der Eigentümerversammlung | Owners' meeting minutes | <ul><li>Must be the most recent, within the last **2 years**.</li><li>Should show the appointed property manager (*Verwalter*) or board members, their roles, and signatures.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
| Verwaltervertrag oder Bestellungsbeschluss | Property manager contract or appointment resolution | <ul><li>Required to confirm who manages the association and has authority to open an account.</li><li>Must be signed and dated.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Vollmacht und Personalausweis des Kontoeröffners | Power of Attorney and identity document of the account opener | <ul><li>Required when the account opener isn't the property manager or a directly authorized board member.</li><li>Must clearly grant account-opening and management powers.</li><li>Must be signed by the correct representative(s) and dated within the last year.</li></ul> | <No /> |
| Personalausweis des gesetzlichen Vertreters | Identity document of the legal representative | <ul><li>Must be a valid identity document in color.</li><li>Both sides, if applicable.</li></ul> | <Yes /> |
