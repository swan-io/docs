# Support team fraud response playbook

When a user reports fraud, your support team is the first point of contact.
Their priority is to **prevent any further loss**, not to investigate.

Follow one principle: **secure, collect, transmit**.

:::caution
Your support team must **never ask users for** their password, MFA code, one-time code, PIN, or full card number.
:::

## Step 1: Secure immediately {#secure}

Ask the user to:

- Stop replying to the fraudster, and stop calling any number the fraudster gave them.
- Stop clicking links, scanning QR codes, or opening attachments the fraudster sent.
- Stop approving operations, and don't make any unusual transfer or payment.
- Change their passwords from official websites or apps. This includes your app, Swan, their email account, and any service where they reused the same password.
- Check that no unknown phone number or email address was added to their account.
- Turn on MFA or 2FA when available.
- Stop using their card if it might be compromised, and [block](../../../topics/cards/physical/guide-block-unblock.mdx) or cancel it.

If the whole account seems compromised, or the user lost access to it, ask Swan to suspend the account.
Submit a [Swan Support request](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new).

## Step 2: Collect the essentials {#collect}

Collect only the first information you need:

- The user and account affected.
- The date and time of the events.
- How the fraudster made contact: email, text message, phone, website, or another channel.
- A short description of what happened.
- What the user shared with the fraudster.
- The transactions, cards, or access involved.
- Actions already taken.
- Evidence: screenshots, emails, text messages, phone numbers, and links.
- Any similar cases you know of.

Don't wait for a complete file before you send the case to Swan.

## Step 3: Transmit to Swan {#transmit}

Report the fraud to Swan as quickly as possible.

- **For completed transactions**, create a [fraud dispute case](/topics/dispute-cases/#fraud) with the **Fraud report** form in your Dashboard, or with the API.
- **If no transaction has gone through** yet, or for an urgent account suspension, submit a [Swan Support request](https://partner-support.swan.io/hc/en-gb/requests/new).

Include:

- The **account ID**, and the **account membership ID** and **card ID** when relevant.
- A summary of the facts and how urgent the situation seems.
- The security measures already taken.
- The information shared with the fraudster.
- The evidence available.
- Anything in common with other cases.
- Any issue, error, or limit you found on your side, and any unusual behavior you noticed.

You can add missing information later.

## After you transmit {#after}

After you send the case, your support team:

- Doesn't investigate.
- Doesn't decide on a refund.
- Doesn't promise the recovery of funds.
- Doesn't ask the user to repeat any operation.
- Only relays information and instructions that Swan validated.

To explain what happens next, see [What happens after a report](./index.mdx#after-report).

### First response to the user {#template-user}

Use this template when a user reports a fraud attempt, an unknown transaction, or a possible compromise of their account or card.

```text
Hello [First name],

Thank you for reporting this situation. We've received your report.

For your security, please:
- Stop replying to the person who contacted you.
- Don't click any link you didn't request.
- Don't approve any new operation you didn't start.
- Change your passwords from official websites or apps.
- Secure the email address linked to your account.
- Block your card if its details might be compromised.
- Tell us about any transaction, change, or login you don't recognize.

Please keep the emails, text messages, screenshots, phone numbers,
and transaction references linked to this incident.

We're sending the first details of your case to Swan for analysis.

Never send us your password, MFA code, PIN, or full card number.

At this stage, we can't confirm the outcome of the analysis or guarantee
a refund. We'll keep you informed through an official channel.

Kind regards,
[Name]
[Company]
```

Don't confirm that fraud is established before Swan's analysis, and don't promise a refund or the recovery of funds.

### New pattern or suspected campaign {#template-pattern}

Use this template to [alert Swan](./index.mdx#new-pattern) when you notice a new fraud scenario, suspicious contact details, several similar reports, or a possible campaign.

```text
Subject: [ALERT] New pattern or suspected campaign

Short description:
[Describe the facts observed]

Channel used:
[Email, text message, phone, website, QR code, other]

Suspicious elements:
- Phone number:
- Email address:
- Link or domain:
- Merchant or beneficiary:
- Amount or transaction:
- Script or request made:

Number of known cases:
[Number or unknown]

Actions already taken:
[Users informed, accounts secured, cards blocked, other]

Evidence available:
[Screenshots, emails, text messages, links, transaction references]
```

Describe only what you observed, and separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
Don't tell users a campaign is confirmed before Swan validates it.

## Rules that always apply {#rules}

- Never send passwords, MFA codes, one-time codes, or full card numbers to Swan.
- Mention any recent transaction or loss of access right away.
- Flag anything in common with other cases.
- Send what you have now, and complete the case later if needed.
