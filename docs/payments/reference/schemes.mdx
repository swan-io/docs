## Card schemes {#cards}

Swan issues Mastercard cards. For [online card acceptance](/payments/concepts/merchants/online-cards#about), Swan supports most major card schemes, including Visa, Mastercard, and Cartes Bancaires (CB) in France; American Express isn't supported. [In-person card acceptance](/payments/concepts/merchants/in-person-cards) runs on the Stripe Terminal SDK.
