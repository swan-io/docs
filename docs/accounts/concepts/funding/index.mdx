# Account funding

<p className="ia-lede">Add money to a Swan account using <Term id="sdd">SEPA Direct Debit</Term> B2B or credit transfers, and track funding sources, <Term id="payment-mandate">payment mandates</Term>, and account verification.</p>

<Term id="account-funding">Account funding</Term> is adding money to your Swan account.
You might use account funding if it's the first time you're sending money to your Swan account, or if you'd like to fund your Swan account on a recurring basis from an account at another financial institution.

A funding source carries three statuses that move together: the [funding source](/accounts/concepts/funding/sources#funding-source-statuses) itself, its [payment mandate](/accounts/concepts/funding/payment-mandates#mandates-statuses), and [account verification](/accounts/concepts/funding/account-verification#account-verification-statuses).
See [interaction between statuses](/accounts/concepts/funding/status-interaction) for the full sequence.
